Intelligence is defined by execution.

Understanding the world is not enough. Synthoid measures embodied intelligence by what a robot can complete in the real world — reliably, safely, and at industrial grade.

Product

A parallel stack, not a single black box.

Industry needs system intelligence. Synthoid’s products run as coordinated layers — decision, world context, adaptation, and the robot body — so each part can be understood, maintained, and improved.

01 · Decision

SaiVLA

A Vision–Language–Action model that aligns perception, language, and action — so robots can follow intent and act in unstructured environments.

02 · Context

SaiWorldModel

An explicit 3D world model for spatial cognition and causal reasoning — geometry, semantics, and physical context the system can reuse over time.

03 · Adaptation

CoRMA

A reinforcement-learning adaptation layer that turns world understanding into precise, recoverable physical execution under contact and disturbance.

04 · Body

SynRobot

A general-purpose robot platform purpose-built for embodied algorithms — coordinating perception, onboard compute, dual-arm action, and mobility.

Approach

Built for industrial reality, not laboratory demos.

Factories, logistics, and service settings are unstructured, long-horizon, and safety-critical. Synthoid designs for those constraints from the first principle.

The problem

Why existing tools stall

Rules-based systems do not scale. Single-task models do not transfer. End-to-end black boxes are hard to explain, debug, or certify when a single error can fail the whole job.

The judgment

System intelligence first

Understanding, decision, and execution are separated with clear responsibility. The stack stays long-term maintainable — and can move from prototype to real-world deployment.

Cognitive evolution

Instruction → intent

From executing commands to understanding context, subtext, and complex human language in the task at hand.

Physical generalization

Factory → open world

From closed production lines to unstructured, changing three-dimensional environments — planning and acting without a script.

Continuous growth

Static → lifelong

From fixed programming to learning through interaction with environments and people — getting more capable with use.

Leadership

Science, systems, and scale — owned by one founding team.

Three accountable owners cover the scientific foundation, the end-to-end architecture, and commercial delivery. Core R&D is graduate-trained, with research mapped to real robot tasks.
