Before breaking down Southeast Asia’s AI landscape for the next decade, let’s first look at a few numbers:

· 690 million people — larger than the European Union (450 million), larger than Latin America (660 million), and about half the size of India’s population, but growing faster.

· 370 million internet users — more than the United States.

· 218 million “digital natives” (Gen Z + Gen Alpha) — one of the youngest and most mobile-internet-native population blocs in the world.

· In 2025, Southeast Asia’s digital economy GMV surpassed US$300 billion — up 15% year-on-year and 1.5 times higher than what was projected a decade ago. It is expected to reach US$600 billion by 2030.

· Southeast Asia’s AI market reached around US$25 billion in 2025 — with data center capacity surging 180% year-on-year and Singapore attracting US$1.31 billion in AI private funding in the first half of the year. By 2030, the market is expected to reach around US$130 billion — a fivefold increase.

Next, we will break it down layer by layer: the full landscape of Southeast Asia’s AI industry, the key players, five major opportunities, three major risks, and what the next decade may look like.

Southeast Asia’s AI Industry Landscape: Infrastructure, Model Layer, and Application Layer

Infrastructure Layer: The Data Center Boom

Southeast Asia is undergoing an unprecedented wave of data center construction — the “new infrastructure” of the AI era.

By 2025, Southeast Asia’s data center capacity had reached around 4,600 MW, up 180% year-on-year and far exceeding previous expectations. By 2030, it is expected to exceed 8,000 MW.

There are three major hotspots:

1. Singapore: As Asia-Pacific’s financial and data hub, Singapore has around 1,300 MW of data center capacity. Due to power constraints, Singapore suspended approvals for new data centers from 2019 to 2022. After reopening approvals in 2022, the review process became much stricter, mainly serving high-end financial institutions and multinational headquarters.

2. Johor, Malaysia (Iskandar): Johor is Singapore’s data center “overflow market.” By 2025, announced or under-construction capacity had exceeded 3,500 MW. It is expected to surpass Singapore in 2026 and become Southeast Asia’s largest data center market.

· Microsoft: Investing US$2.2 billion to build hyperscale cloud infrastructure.

· Google: Investing US$2 billion to build data centers and a cloud region.

· Amazon AWS: Investing US$6 billion to build a Kuala Lumpur cloud region.

· ByteDance: Investing US$2.1 billion in Johor to build AI and TikTok computing infrastructure.

· Oracle, NVIDIA, Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud: All are making large-scale deployments in Johor.

3. Indonesia: Jakarta–Batam

· Microsoft: Announced in April 2024 that it would invest US$1.7 billion to build cloud infrastructure in Indonesia, with implementation advancing in 2025.

· Google: Announced a US$2 billion investment to build an AI center in Indonesia.

· AWS: Investing US$5 billion to build a Jakarta cloud region.

· Alibaba Cloud, Huawei Cloud, and Tencent Cloud: All have already deployed infrastructure locally.

Why these three places?

Because power, water, land, submarine cables, and political stability are the five essential conditions for AI infrastructure. In Southeast Asia, only Singapore, Johor, and Jakarta can meet these requirements at the same time.

Network and Connectivity Layer

· Submarine cables: Southeast Asia is one of the most densely connected submarine cable regions in the world. New-generation cables such as SeaMeWe-6, Apricot, and Echo all position Southeast Asia as a core node.

· 5G: Singapore and Malaysia have reached nearly 100% coverage. Thailand, Indonesia, and the Philippines are at around 70–90%. Vietnam only commercialized 5G in 2024 and is accelerating deployment in 2025, making it a late mover.

· Digital identity: Singapore’s Singpass, Indonesia’s KTP, Thailand’s ThaID, and Malaysia’s MyDigital ID — Southeast Asia’s digital identity systems are rapidly improving and becoming the infrastructure for AI application deployment.

Model Layer: Localization and Multilingual AI

The biggest challenge for Southeast Asia’s AI model layer is multilingualism and multicultural complexity.

· Bahasa Indonesia: 280 million users.

· Vietnamese: 100 million users.

· Thai: 70 million users.

· Malay: 33 million users, closely related to Indonesian.

· Tagalog: 28 million users.

· Burmese, Khmer, and Lao: 80 million users combined.

Together with local dialects and Chinese-speaking communities, Southeast Asia is one of the most linguistically complex regions in the world.

No global foundation model performs exceptionally well across all Southeast Asian languages.

That is exactly the opportunity for localized large language models.

The main local model initiatives in Southeast Asia include:

· Singapore’s SEA-LION, initiated by AI Singapore: Launched in 2023, covering 11 Southeast Asian languages, and recognized as Southeast Asia’s first open-source multilingual LLM.

· MERaLiON, developed through collaboration between AI Singapore and IMDA: Launched in 2024 and upgraded in 2025 to cover 10 major Southeast Asian languages.

· PhoGPT by VinAI / VinGroup: Vietnam’s local LLM.

· OpenThaiGPT and Typhoon: Thailand’s localized LLMs.

· Tagalog-Llama: A local variant based on Meta’s open-source model.

Application Layer: The Real Battlefield for Southeast Asia’s AI Boom

The application layer is where Southeast Asia’s AI value has the greatest imagination.

Infrastructure and foundation models are already dominated by Chinese and American tech giants, but the application layer remains open, unlimited, and deeply local.

The core directions of AI applications in Southeast Asia include:

1. AI customer service and BPO upgrade: The Philippine BPO industry is worth US$38 billion a year, and AI customer service technology is reshaping the sector.

2. AI e-commerce: TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada have all deployed AI recommendations, AI content generation, and AI advertising at scale.

3. AI finance: Grab, GoTo, and SeaMoney are using AI for risk control, credit, and robo-advisory.

4. AI content: AI-generated content for short videos, livestreaming, and gaming in Southeast Asia.

5. AI education: AI teachers from Indonesia’s Ruangguru, Singapore’s Tencent Education, and the Philippines’ Edukasyon.

6. AI healthcare: Singapore’s SingHealth collaboration with Tencent Healthcare, Indonesia’s Halodoc, and Vietnam’s Vinmec in AI imaging diagnostics.

7. AI agriculture: Precision agriculture, pest recognition, and climate forecasting in Indonesia, Vietnam, and Thailand.

Five AI Opportunity Tracks

Track 1: Multilingual Foundation Models and Localization

Market size: By 2030, Southeast Asia’s multilingual AI model market is expected to reach US$15 billion.

Core opportunities:

Indonesian LLMs: Indonesia is the largest single market, with a population of 280 million. The first foundation model that can bring Bahasa Indonesia performance close to English-level quality will gain a major first-mover advantage.

Vietnamese LLMs: Vietnam has one of the fastest-growing digital economies, and demand for Vietnamese LLMs is urgent.

Thai LLMs: Thai is technically difficult, with tones and special characters, making it a high-barrier niche market.

Unified Southeast Asian multilingual models: A model that can work across 10 Southeast Asian languages has the chance to become Southeast Asia’s version of “GPT.”

Key players:

China: DeepSeek, with the strongest open-source ecosystem; Qwen, Alibaba’s core model for Southeast Asia; Doubao from ByteDance; Kimi from Moonshot AI; Zhipu AI; and Baidu’s ERNIE Bot.

United States: OpenAI GPT, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, and Meta Llama.

Local: SEA-LION, PhoGPT, Typhoon, and others.

Advantages of Chinese players:

1. Open-source ecosystem: DeepSeek, Qwen, and ChatGLM are all open source, allowing Southeast Asian developers to deploy and fine-tune models locally while avoiding cross-border data compliance issues.

2. Chinese–Southeast Asian language bridge: Southeast Asia has 30 million ethnic Chinese. The demand for two-way translation and conversation between Chinese and Southeast Asian languages is huge.

3. Price advantage: Chinese foundation model APIs are often only one-tenth the price of OpenAI — a critical factor for SMEs and application developers in Southeast Asia.

Track 2: AI-Driven E-commerce and Local Services

Market size: By 2030, Southeast Asia’s e-commerce GMV is expected to reach US$400 billion, with AI-driven transactions accounting for more than 60%.

Core opportunities:

AI customer service: From chatbots to full-process AI sales assistants.

AI content generation: Automated product descriptions, product images, and short videos.

AI recommendation and personalization: Moving from simple collaborative filtering to deep personalized marketing.

AI livestreaming: Virtual hosts, real-time translated livestreams, and AI digital humans for commerce.

AI advertising: Automated ad creatives and precision targeting.

Key players:

TikTok Shop: ByteDance’s AI recommendation and content generation capabilities have already been deployed at scale across Southeast Asia. TikTok Shop is the most aggressive AI e-commerce laboratory in the region.

Shopee (Sea Group): Self-developed AI algorithms combined with Tencent AI capabilities.

Lazada (Alibaba): Powered by Qwen and Alibaba Cloud’s full-stack AI capabilities.

Temu (PDD overseas): Ultra-low prices combined with AI algorithms. By 2025, monthly active users in Southeast Asia had surpassed 35 million.

SHEIN: Its AI-driven “small-batch, fast-response” supply chain is its core moat.

Track 3: AI Finance and Digital Banking

Market size: Among Southeast Asia’s 690 million people, around 240 million adults remain unbanked — one of the world’s largest financially underserved populations. AI risk control and digital banking can serve these users at extremely low cost.

Core opportunities:

AI credit: Alternative credit scoring based on mobile data, social data, and e-commerce data.

AI risk control: Anti-fraud, anti-money laundering, and compliance automation.

AI robo-advisory: Intelligent wealth management for Southeast Asia’s emerging middle class.

AI cross-border payments: Overseas Filipino worker remittances and cross-border e-commerce settlement.

AI insurance: Insurance penetration in Southeast Asia remains low, at only around 3–5%, leaving huge room for AI-driven microinsurance.

Key players:

Grab Financial: One of Southeast Asia’s largest fintech platforms, backed by Tencent and SoftBank.

GoTo Financial (Indonesia): Includes GoPay and GoPayLater, with Alibaba as a shareholder.

SeaMoney (Sea Group): Tencent-backed and one of Southeast Asia’s major digital wallet players.

GCash (Mynt): The largest e-wallet in the Philippines, with Ant Group as a major shareholder.

TrueMoney (Thailand): Backed by Alibaba and Ant Group.

Tonik, Tyme, UnionDigital: Southeast Asia’s new digital banks.

Track 4: AI Education and AI Healthcare

Education market size: Southeast Asia’s K–12 education market was around US$22 billion in 2025, with AI education penetration at about 8%. By 2030, the AI education market is expected to reach US$8 billion.

Healthcare market size: Southeast Asia’s healthcare spending was around US$290 billion in 2025. The AI healthcare market is expected to reach US$15 billion by 2030.

Core opportunities:

AI English learning: The Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia, and Thailand all have massive demand for English learning.

AI exam preparation: AI question banks for Indonesia’s SBMPTN, Vietnam’s national college entrance exam, and Thailand’s TCAS.

AI imaging diagnostics: AI radiology for hospitals in Thailand, Indonesia, and Vietnam.

AI telemedicine: Halodoc in Indonesia, Doctor Anywhere in Singapore, and Jio Health in Vietnam.

AI drug delivery: Integration with e-commerce and logistics.

Key players:

Ruangguru (Indonesia): Southeast Asia’s largest online education platform.

Yuanfudao, TAL, NetEase Youdao: Chinese education AI companies expanding into Southeast Asia.

Halodoc, Alodokter, Doctor Anywhere, Jio Health: Representative AI healthcare companies in the region.

Ping An Good Doctor and DXY: Chinese healthcare AI players exploring Southeast Asia.

Track 5: AI Government and Public Services

Market size: Government digitalization budgets across Southeast Asia totaled around US$17.5 billion in 2025 and are expected to reach US$50 billion by 2030, with AI accounting for around 30%.

Core opportunities:

AI customer service: Government hotlines, social security inquiries, and tax Q&A.

AI identity authentication: Facial recognition, voiceprint authentication, and digital identity.

AI intelligent transportation: Bangkok, Jakarta, and Manila are among the world’s most congested cities.

AI smart cities: From Singapore’s “Smart Nation” to the digitalization of Johor, Jakarta, and Hanoi.

AI national governance: Indonesia’s new capital, Nusantara, is planned as one of the world’s most advanced AI-driven smart cities.

Key players:

AI Singapore, Indonesia’s Telkomsel, Malaysia’s MyDigital, Thailand’s DEPA, and Vietnam’s MIC: National-level government digitalization agencies.

Huawei: One of the largest smart city solution providers in Southeast Asia, with deployments in Singapore, Jakarta, Kuala Lumpur, Manila, and Bangkok.

Hikvision, Dahua, SenseTime, Megvii, and Yitu: Chinese AI vision companies expanding across Southeast Asia.

Microsoft, Google, and AWS: Core providers of government cloud infrastructure.

Three Risks Facing Southeast Asia’s AI Industry

Risk 1: Geopolitical Decoupling

· U.S. export restrictions on AI chips to China, especially high-end NVIDIA chips, are affecting Southeast Asia, as regulators worry that some AI companies may obtain compute through Southeast Asian channels.

· NVIDIA chip re-exports through Malaysia and Singapore have already attracted scrutiny from the U.S. Department of Commerce since 2024, with controls tightening further in 2025.

Risk 2: Data Localization and Compliance

· Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law, which came into effect in October 2024; Singapore’s PDPA; Vietnam’s data protection law, effective in 2025 — Southeast Asian countries are rapidly building data protection frameworks.

· Indonesia and Vietnam require critical data in finance, government, healthcare, and other sectors to be stored locally, increasing compliance costs for AI companies.

· Indonesia’s TikTok Shop ban in September 2023, the Philippines’ POGO ban in 2024, Malaysia’s Bumiputera equity requirements, and Indonesia’s 40% localization requirement for foreign investment in 2025 are all sensitive events worth watching.

Risk 3: Insufficient Depth of Localization

· The biggest weakness for AI companies expanding into Southeast Asia remains “localization depth” — organizational depth, language gaps, brand tone, local perspective, and community acceptance. This will be a key test over the next five years.

What Does Southeast Asia Mean for Chinese AI?

It is the nearest testing ground for globalization — short flight distances, cultural proximity, strong Chinese community networks, and high willingness from governments to cooperate.

It is a training ground for multilingual capability — if a foundation model can hold natural conversations in Bahasa Indonesia, Vietnamese, Thai, Malay, and Tagalog, it has achieved true globalization capability.

It is fertile ground for application-layer innovation — 690 million people, 370 million internet users, and more than 200 million digital natives make Southeast Asia an ideal market for AI product-market fit validation.

It is a sensitive frontline of geopolitical competition — chips, data, and compliance all place Southeast Asia at the center of the AI contest.

For Chinese companies, Chinese capital, and Chinese talent, Southeast Asia is:

· The first stop for global expansion.

· A training ground for globalization.

· A place to learn multicultural execution.

· A new blue ocean in the AI era.

Winning Southeast Asia does not require only “Chinese speed.”

It requires “Chinese depth.”

It means Chinese companies must make a real shift: from exporting products to exporting systems, from selling technology to building ecosystems.

接下来，我们逐层拆解：东南亚AI产业的全景、核心玩家、五个机会、三个风险，以及未来十年的可能图景。

东南亚AI产业全景：基础设施、模型层、应用层

基础设施层：数据中心的爆发

东南亚正在经历一场前所未有的数据中心建设浪潮——这是AI时代的”新基建”。

2025年东南亚数据中心容量已达约4600兆瓦（同比激增180%，超过此前预期），预计2030年突破8000兆瓦。三个热点区域：

1.新加坡：作为亚太金融与数据枢纽，新加坡数据中心容量约1300兆瓦。但因电力紧张，2019-2022年新加坡曾暂停审批新数据中心。2022年解禁后审批严格，主要服务高端金融、跨国总部需求。

2.马来西亚柔佛（依斯干达）：是新加坡数据中心的”溢出地”——2025年宣布或在建容量超过3500兆瓦，预计2026年即超越新加坡，成为东南亚最大数据中心市场。

·微软：投资22亿美元建超大规模云。

·谷歌：投资20亿美元建数据中心+云区域。

·亚马逊AWS：投资60亿美元建吉隆坡云区域。

·字节跳动：在柔佛投资21亿美元建AI/TikTok计算基础设施。

·甲骨文、英伟达、阿里云、华为云、腾讯云：均在柔佛大规模布局。

3.印尼雅加达—巴淡岛：

·微软：2024年4月宣布投资17亿美元建印尼云，2025年项目落地推进。

·谷歌：宣布投资20亿美元建印尼AI中心。

·AWS：投资50亿美元建雅加达云区域。

·阿里云、华为云、腾讯云：均已部署。

为什么是这三个地方？电力+水+土地+海底光缆+政治稳定——这五个条件在东南亚同时具备的地方，只有新加坡、柔佛、雅加达。

网络与连接层

·海底光缆：东南亚是全球海底光缆密度最高的区域之一。SeaMeWe-6（2025年开通）、Apricot（2024年开通）、Echo等新一代光缆都把东南亚作为核心节点。

·5G：新加坡、马来西亚已100%覆盖；泰国、印尼、菲律宾覆盖率约70-90%；越南2024年才商用，2025年加速扩张，是后发国。

·数字身份：新加坡Singpass、印尼KTP、泰国ThaID、马来西亚MyDigital ID——东南亚的数字身份系统快速完善，是AI应用落地的”基础设施”。

模型层：本地化与多语言

东南亚AI模型层最大的挑战是多语言+多文化：

·印尼语（Bahasa Indonesia）：2.8亿使用者。

·越南语：1亿使用者。

·泰语：7000万使用者。

·马来语：3300万使用者（与印尼语接近）。

·他加禄语（Tagalog）：2800万使用者。

·缅甸语、柬埔寨语、老挝语：合计8000万使用者。

加上各国的方言和华语社区，东南亚是世界上语言最复杂的区域之一。没有一个全球大模型能在所有东南亚语言中表现优异——这也是”本地化大模型”的机会。

东南亚本地大模型的主要尝试：

·新加坡SEA-LION（由AI Singapore发起）：2023年推出，覆盖11种东南亚语言，是东南亚首个开源多语言LLM。

·印尼MERaLiON（由新加坡AI Singapore + IMDA合作）：2024年推出，2025年升级覆盖东南亚10种主要语言。

·越南VinAI（VinGroup）的PhoGPT：越南本土LLM。

·泰国OpenThaiGPT、Typhoon：泰国本地化LLM。

·菲律宾Tagalog-Llama：基于Meta开源的本地变体。

应用层：东南亚AI爆发的真正战场

应用层才是东南亚AI价值最有想象空间的部分——基础设施和模型已经被中美科技巨头主导，但应用层是开放的、无限的、本地化的。

东南亚AI应用的核心方向：

1.AI客服与BPO升级：菲律宾BPO行业380亿美元/年——AI客服技术正在重塑这个行业。

2.AI电商：TikTok Shop、Shopee、Lazada都已大规模部署AI推荐、AI内容生成、AI广告。

3.AI金融：Grab、GoTo、SeaMoney的AI风控、AI信贷、AI投顾。

4.AI内容：东南亚短视频、直播、游戏的AI生成。

5.AI教育：印尼Ruangguru、新加坡Tencent Education、菲律宾Edukasyon的AI教师。

6.AI医疗：新加坡SingHealth与腾讯医疗合作；印尼Halodoc；越南Vinmec的AI影像诊断。

7.AI农业：印尼、越南、泰国的精准农业、病虫害识别、气候预测。

五个AI机会赛道

赛道1：多语言大模型与本地化

市场规模：到2030年，东南亚多语言AI模型市场预计达到150亿美元。

核心机会： 

印尼语LLM：印尼是单一最大市场（2.8亿人口），第一个能把印尼语能力做到与英语相当的大模型，将占据先机。 

越南语LLM：越南数字经济增长最快，越南语LLM紧迫需求大。 

泰语LLM：泰国语言难度高（声调+特殊字符），是技术壁垒高的细分市场。

东南亚多语言一体化：能跨10种东南亚语言的大模型，有机会成为东南亚版的”GPT”。

核心玩家： 

中国：DeepSeek（开源生态最强）、Qwen（阿里巴巴在东南亚的核心模型）、Doubao（字节跳动）、Kimi（月之暗面）、智谱AI、文心一言（百度）。 

美国：OpenAI GPT、Anthropic Claude、Google Gemini、Meta Llama（开源）。 

本地：SEA-LION、PhoGPT、Typhoon等。

中国玩家优势： 

1.开源生态：DeepSeek、Qwen、ChatGLM都是开源——东南亚开发者可以本地部署、本地微调，避开数据出境合规问题。 

2.中文-东南亚语言桥梁：东南亚有3000万华人，中文与东南亚语言的双向翻译/对话需求巨大。 

3. 价格优势：中国大模型API价格通常仅为OpenAI的1/10——对东南亚的中小企业和应用开发者至关重要。

赛道2：AI驱动的电商与本地生活

市场规模：到2030年，东南亚电商GMV预计达到4000亿美元——其中AI驱动的部分预计占60%以上。

核心机会： 

AI客服：从聊天机器人到全流程AI销售助理。 

AI内容生成：商品文案、产品图、短视频自动生成。 

AI推荐与个性化：从单纯的”协同过滤”到深度个性化营销。 

AI直播：虚拟主播、实时翻译直播、AI数字人带货。 

AI广告：自动化广告创意+精准投放。

核心玩家： 

TikTok Shop：字节跳动的AI推荐和内容生成已经在东南亚大规模部署，TikTok Shop是AI电商最激进的实验室。 

Shopee（Sea Group）：自研AI算法+腾讯AI能力。 

Lazada（阿里）：通义千问+阿里云全栈AI能力赋能。 

Temu（拼多多海外）：极致价格+AI算法，2025年东南亚月活已突破3500万。 

Shein：AI驱动的”小单快反”供应链是其核心壁垒。

赛道3：AI金融与数字银行

市场规模：东南亚6.9亿人口中，仍有约2.4亿”无银行账户”成年人——这是全球最大的”金融未覆盖人群”之一。AI风控+数字银行可以以极低的成本服务这些人群。

核心机会： 

AI信贷：基于手机数据、社交数据、电商数据的另类信用评分。 

AI风控：反欺诈、反洗钱、合规自动化。 

AI投顾：面向东南亚新中产的智能理财。 

AI跨境支付：OFW汇款、跨境电商结算。 

AI保险：东南亚保险渗透率低（仅约3-5%），AI驱动的微保险有巨大空间。

核心玩家： 

Grab Financial：东南亚最大金融科技平台之一，腾讯+软银投资。 

GoTo Financial（印尼）：包括GoPay、GoPayLater，阿里巴巴持股。 

SeaMoney（Sea Group）：腾讯系，东南亚第三大数字钱包。 

GCash（Mynt）：菲律宾第一大电子钱包，蚂蚁集团是大股东。 

TrueMoney（泰国）：阿里巴巴+蚂蚁系。 

Tonik、Tyme、UnionDigital：东南亚新数字银行。

赛道4：AI教育与AI医疗

教育市场规模：东南亚K-12教育市场2025年约220亿美元，AI教育渗透率约8%——预计2030年AI教育市场将达到80亿美元。

医疗市场规模：东南亚医疗支出2025年约2900亿美元——AI医疗预计2030年将达到150亿美元。

核心机会： 

AI英语教学：菲律宾、越南、印尼、泰国都有巨大的英语学习需求。 

AI考试辅导：印尼SBMPTN、越南高考、泰国TCAS的AI题库。 

AI影像诊断：泰国、印尼、越南的医院AI放射科。 

AI在线问诊：Halodoc（印尼）、Doctor Anywhere（新加坡）、Jio Health（越南）。 

AI药品配送：与电商和物流结合。

核心玩家： 

Ruangguru（印尼）：东南亚最大的在线教育平台。 

猿辅导、好未来、网易有道：中国教育AI企业出海东南亚。 

Halodoc、Alodokter（印尼）、Doctor Anywhere（新加坡）、Jio Health（越南）：AI医疗代表。 

平安好医生、丁香园：中国医疗AI出海尝试。

赛道5：AI政府与公共服务

市场规模：东南亚各国政府数字化预算2025年合计约175亿美元——预计2030年达到500亿美元，其中AI部分占比约30%。

核心机会： 

AI客服：政务热线、社保咨询、税务问答。 

AI身份认证：人脸识别、声纹认证、电子身份。 

AI智能交通：曼谷、雅加达、马尼拉的城市交通是世界上最堵之一。 

AI智慧城市：从新加坡的”智慧国家”到柔佛、雅加达、河内的城市数字化。 

AI国家治理：印尼新首都”努桑塔拉”（Nusantara）规划为全球最先进的AI驱动智慧城市之一。

核心玩家： 

新加坡AI Singapore、印尼Telkomsel、马来西亚MyDigital、泰国DEPA、越南MIC——各国政府数字化机构。 

华为：东南亚智慧城市最大解决方案商之一，新加坡、雅加达、吉隆坡、马尼拉、曼谷都有部署。 

海康威视、大华、商汤、旷视、依图：中国AI视觉企业的东南亚布局。 

微软、谷歌、AWS：政府云的核心客户。

中国AI企业出海东南亚的”四个梯队”

第一梯队：基础设施层（云+模型）

·阿里云（Alibaba Cloud）：东南亚最大的中国云厂商，覆盖新加坡、雅加达、吉隆坡、曼谷、马尼拉等数据中心。Lazada+TikTok之外的第三大客户群。

·华为云：东南亚智慧城市、政府云的最大供应商之一。

·腾讯云：游戏、视频、Sea Group生态的核心云。

·字节跳动云（Volcengine）：从TikTok向外延伸的AI云。

·DeepSeek：开源模型在东南亚开发者社区影响巨大——2024年Q4起，东南亚使用DeepSeek的初创团队呈现爆发式增长，2025年R1/V3模型成为东南亚开发者首选开源底座。

第二梯队：应用层巨头

·TikTok（字节跳动）：东南亚3亿月活，是中国互联网企业海外最成功的案例。

·Shopee（腾讯系）：东南亚最大电商。

·Lazada（阿里）：覆盖六国电商。

·Temu（拼多多）：2024年快速崛起，2025年东南亚月活突破3500万。

·SHEIN：总部已迁至新加坡。

·米哈游、莉莉丝、网易、腾讯游戏：游戏出海。

第三梯队：垂直AI应用创业

·AI客服与RPA：UB Tech、智齿科技、容联云通讯等中国AI客服公司在菲律宾、印尼有大量客户。

·AI视觉：海康威视、大华、商汤、旷视的安防+智慧城市方案。

·AI教育：网易有道、好未来在东南亚的产品布局。

·AI医疗：平安好医生、丁香园、医联的尝试。

·AI农业：极飞科技在泰国、越南的农业无人机。

·AI物流：菜鸟、京东物流的智能仓储+智能配送。

第四梯队：硬件AI

·大疆无人机：消费级和企业级双线。

·小米、华为、OPPO、vivo：AI手机生态。

·Insta360（影石）：全景相机出海代表。

·石头科技、追觅、科沃斯：AI扫地机器人。

东南亚各国的AI战略

新加坡：亚太AI总部

·国家AI战略2.0（2023年12月发布）：目标培养1.5万AI专业人才，到2030年AI对GDP贡献达到180亿新元。

·AI Verify Foundation：全球第一个AI治理开源工具，由新加坡发起。

·AI Singapore：国家AI研究中心，发起SEA-LION、MERaLiON等项目。

·FinTech Festival金融科技节：每年11月在新加坡举办，全球最大金融科技+AI盛会之一。

·Singapore AI Week：6月在新加坡举办，是新加坡最大的AI专业活动，企业出海平台AGI Villa发起的GO SUMMIT Singapore作为中国和亚太连接的桥梁，是其重要组成部份（www.luma.com/agivilla）。

印尼：东南亚最大AI市场

·国家AI战略2020-2045：长期愿景，目标AI对印尼GDP贡献达到3660亿美元。

·新首都Nusantara：规划为全球最先进的AI驱动智慧城市之一。

·印尼数字部（Komdigi）：2024年新成立的部委，2025年深度统筹AI、数据、网络治理。

·印尼数据中心爆发：阿里云、AWS、谷歌、微软都已落地。

马来西亚：AI节点+数据中心枢纽

·MyDigital战略（2021-2030年）：目标培养50万数字工程师。

·国家AI路线图（AI-RMAP）：2021年发布。

·MIMOS（国家研究机构）：是马来西亚AI研发的核心。

·柔佛数据中心：东南亚AI算力的核心节点。

泰国：AI制造+AI旅游

·泰国4.0战略（2017-至今）：目标推动制造业、旅游业的AI化。

·DEPA（数字经济促进局）：是泰国AI生态的核心机构。

·AI for Thai：本地化LLM项目。

越南：AI制造+AI国家

·国家数字化转型计划（到2030年）：目标AI对GDP贡献达到12%。

·VinAI（VinGroup）、FPT.AI、Viettel AI：三大本土AI龙头。

·越南AI民族企业：富士通+越南国营电信合作。

菲律宾：AI BPO升级

·国家AI路线图（NAIR，2021年发布）：目标推动BPO行业从”人工”到”AI增强”。

·DICT（信息通信技术部）：核心机构。

·菲律宾BPO的AI升级是亚洲最大的”AI改造存量行业”案例之一。

东南亚AI产业的”三个风险”

风险1：地缘脱钩

·美国对中国AI芯片出口限制（特别是英伟达高端芯片）正在影响东南亚——其担心部分AI企业绕道东南亚获得算力。

·马来西亚、新加坡的英伟达芯片转口：2024年美国商务部已经发起调查，2025年继续加强管控。

风险2：数据本地化与合规

·印尼个人数据保护法（2024年10月生效）、新加坡PDPA、越南数据保护法（2025年生效）等——东南亚各国都在快速建立数据保护法律。

·印尼、越南要求”金融、政务、医疗等关键数据必须本地存储”——这增加了AI企业的合规成本。

·2023年9月印尼禁TikTok Shop、2024年菲律宾禁POGO、马来西亚要求Bumiputera股权、2025年印尼外资本地化40%要求——这些都是值得关注的敏感事件。

风险3：本地化深度不足

·AI企业出海东南亚的最大短板仍然是“本地化深度”——组织深度、语言Gap、品牌调性、本地视角、社区接受度等，这是未来5年的关键考验。

东南亚之于中国AI，意味着什么？

它是最近的全球化试验田——飞行距离短、文化相似度高、华人渠道广、政府合作意愿强。

它是多语言能力的练兵场——能在印尼语、越南语、泰语、马来语、他加禄语都做到自然对话的大模型，意味着真正的”全球化能力”。

它是应用层创新的沃土——6.9亿人口、3.7亿网民、2亿数字原生代——是AI应用产品的绝佳PMF验证场。

它是地缘博弈的敏感前线——芯片、数据、合规，每一个议题都让东南亚成为AI博弈的中心。

对中国企业、中国资本、中国人才而言，东南亚是：

·出海的第一站。

·全球化的练兵场。

·多元文化的学习地。

·AI时代的新蓝海。

赢得东南亚，需要的不是”中国速度”，而是”中国深度”——是中国企业从”输出产品”到”输出体系”，从”卖技术”到”建生态”的真正全球化转身。
