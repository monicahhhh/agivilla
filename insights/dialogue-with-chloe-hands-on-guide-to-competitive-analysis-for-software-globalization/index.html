Finding pain points and opportunities from competitor user reviews

Chloe

If you also want to enter the AI agent or AI design space, where can you find opportunities? Generally, we look for opportunities in user reviews. However, many people who might not be very familiar with overseas markets might not know which platforms to use to filter reviews, or how to distinguish between fake reviews and genuine user reviews.

Here, I've listed some based on my own experience:

For example, there's Reddit. User reviews there tend to be more authentic, but they can be scattered. Users might discuss things unrelated to your product, so it can take some time to find what you need. But if you're good with technology, you can use workflows to filter them.

The second option is platforms like Trustpilot / G2. The biggest advantage of reviews on these platforms is that they can be easily collected centrally. For example, you can use tools like Octoparse (Bazhua Yu) – just put in the URL, and it can help you extract the reviewer, specific review date, rating, score, etc., making it convenient for later filtering in a spreadsheet. After selecting these, the negative reviews in this category tend to be more authentic, so we can directly identify them. Another option might be app stores. If you're developing an app or a plugin, there are many user reviews directly in the app store, and these are also easy to collect.

You can also look at the comments below their promotional YouTube videos or Twitter posts. Finally, there's Product Hunt. Product Hunt's reviews were probably more authentic five or six years ago, but now it's mostly commercial back-patting, so its value isn't as significant.

Ranking them, I would say Trustpilot and G2 come first because their reviews are easy to collect, or app stores. Next would be Reddit, or comments under influencers' YouTube and SNS posts. Product Hunt would be last.

Now, once we have these user reviews, I think the first thing is not to rely too much on AI to summarize them for you, to find opportunities from these comments. How to find your competitors?

Chloe

Let's continue by discussing how to find competitors through research.

Using Higgsfield as an example, its recent performance has been very impressive. It's an AI video generation product, with 17 million traffic in October, up from just over 8 million in August. It's actually quite simple. On one hand, if you already have clear competitors, you can just search on Google, for example, "Higgsfield AI alternatives." Higgsfield sounds like a name from Star Wars, so you'd have to add "AI" and then "alternative" to find the product.

When naming your own product, if you don't have strong backing, avoid using common, overlapping terms. Choose unique names that haven't been used before. This also makes it easier for you to track and see the effectiveness of your brand.

Besides Google, you can also ask ChatGPT or any other AI-type product. It will give you some suggestions, but they might not be accurate. Collect all these. Or, if you've run ads, you can see which domains are competing with you for the same keywords, and those are also your competitors.

Also, if you know who your competitors are, you can use these methods. If you don't know your competitors, for example, you have a need or a scenario, and you don't know if anyone is doing it, you can directly ask on Reddit, finding the relevant subreddit.

Monica

我们继续来讲一下通过调研如何找到竞争对手？

Chloe

以Higgsfield作为例子，它最近成绩非常亮眼，它是一个AI video generation产品，10月份时流量有1,700万，而在8月份时才800多万，也就说一个月时间增长了一倍，这产品也非常值得研究，我就借着这个产品找你的竞争对手。

其实这个很简单，一方面如果你已经有比较明确竞对，那你就直接在Google里去搜搜,比如Higgsfield AI alternatives，Higgsfield好像是星球大战里面名字，你必须要在后面加一个AI，再加alternative，才会是这个产品。

对于我们自己在给产品取名字时，你实力不是那么雄厚的话，千万不要取一些通用词重合的名字，要取一些没有人用过的名字，也方便你自己去回溯你这个品牌做效果能够看出来

除了谷歌外，你也可以问chatgpt，或其他任何AI类型产品，它会给你一些建议，但不一定准确，把这些都搜集起来。你可以到一些平台上面去找，比如ProductHunt，可以看到右侧会给你推荐，另外如果你是一些APP的话，它会在下方也会有related APPS推荐。

像我们刚提到的Trustpilot这些平台，你只要输入你对标产品，你都可以看到一些其他推荐。再一个就是像我们提到的Semrush这样产品，有告诉你在哪些关键词上面跟你是竞争关系的，在SEO渠道上面也会是你竞争对手，或你投放了广告，在这里看到有哪些域名跟你是在同竞争同样关键词的，那也是你竞品。

另外如果你知道你自己竞品是谁时，你可以用到这样方式，如果你不知道自己的竞品，比如你有一个需求，有一个场景，你不知道市面上有没有人在做这件事时，你就可以在Reddit里直接问，找到对应板块,

我们就直接在Reddit提问，假装自己是老师。
