Basic Concepts and Misconceptions of GEO

The biggest characteristic I found through experimentation is that GEO requires a broader and more comprehensive coverage than traditional SEO. It builds upon SEO by adding new brand-related data sources and relevant promotional marketing activities, which helps GEO perform better. I think this is the biggest difference from SEO. Of course, this is just a difference at the operational level. In essence, the concept of GEO is that generative AI traffic will gradually increase, and traditional search engine traffic will gradually decrease. The role of GEO is to enable generative AI to recommend our products and brands, to make our brands appear within these large language models, and to encourage users to choose our brands, thereby achieving this goal. Therefore, all the actions we take are aimed at increasing the probability of large models recommending our brands when providing answers, enhancing brand visibility.

Regarding common misconceptions, the first one is that when GEO emerged, many people said that the era of SEO was over and the future belonged to GEO. However, the reality is that at this stage, SEO is the infrastructure for GEO. This is because the data used to train large models, as well as the data sources they cite for real-time searches, largely come from content on websites, as well as social media, communities, blogs, videos, and other content. So, traditional SEO content is essentially the foundational information source for the GEO era. Furthermore, I personally understand GEO as a continuation of SEO; it just involves doing more things on top of SEO, which I will share later. So, I'll put forward a point of view: GEO is a continuation of SEO. They both aim for the same goal, but at different stages. SEO is the first stage, and GEO is the next stage. Their goal is for our brand to be seen and chosen.

I don't think it's about replacement yet, because the current volume of GEO is still far smaller than SEO. I have some data to show later; basically, I've seen data showing a gap of 10 times, or even more than 30 times. This means SEO traffic is still significantly larger than the traffic brought by GEO, but GEO traffic is continuously increasing. As Monica just shared, it's predicted that SEO traffic will significantly decrease in 2025. We've also seen some predictions, even suggesting that GEO traffic will surpass SEO traffic by 2027. However, at this stage, SEO is the main battlefield, and GEO is a tactical high ground; it's the future, but the main battlefield is still with SEO.

The second misconception is that after the concept of GEO emerged, people felt that if they did GEO well, they could turn things around? If SEO hadn't worked out before, embracing GEO now could change their destiny. The reality is not like that either. First, there's a significant gap in traffic itself; it's not just a big gap compared to SEO traffic, but also very small compared to other traditional channels like advertising and influencer marketing. So, at this stage, I think we should be laying the groundwork for GEO, which is equivalent to building a brand for the AI era. It might not immediately bring good traffic and returns, but it can prepare for the future, especially by letting AI know us and establishing a recommendation mindset for our brand in our respective fields in advance.

The third misconception is that people often hear about tips, methods, and strategies for doing GEO, but these are all rather one-sided. For example, some say to post content on Reddit and Quora, or extensively on Medium, or leave comments in blog sections, or add technical files like LLMs.txt to websites for large model crawlers to access and read. These strategies are all fine, but they are specific tactical points. To do it systematically, it actually involves implementing more things across other dimensions, so it's a systematic project.

I think to do GEO well now, there are several key points:

The first is brand mentions, which can be seen as the equivalent of link building in the SEO era for the AI age. Our brand appearing on the internet now is like getting an external link from another website in the SEO era. This is the most important evaluation dimension influencing how large models recommend our brand.

The second is our own content, or content published on third-party platforms. This content needs to be written in an AI-friendly way, meaning its semantics and structure should be easily understandable by machines and readily citable.

The third is that we not only need brand mentions and exposure, but also need to strongly bind the product brand to the desired usage scenario during exposure. What does this mean? For example, if we have an AI tool that generates ad creatives for YouTube creators or e-commerce sellers. When we do external brand mentions, we should describe our brand, say brand AA, as the best e-commerce ad video creative generation tool; or say it can help e-commerce marketing personnel improve ad generation efficiency and increase ad conversion rates. Such specific scenarios, combined with the brand, should be constantly bound together. This binding should happen not only off-site but also on-site with similar descriptions. When a large amount of such data is read by AI and these large models, they can establish an association between the brand and e-commerce sellers or ad generation tools. Then, no matter what a user asks in a large model, as long as it's related to e-commerce ad creative generation, the brand is likely to be recommended.

The fourth major influencing factor is user discussion data, meaning whether the brand is mentioned on Reddit, YouTube, Facebook, or on review platforms like Trustpilot, or in the App Store or Google Play Store. Are people discussing it? Are they saying good things or bad things? Are there likes, shares, or other actions? These signals will be captured by large models for training, and it's very likely that these are their criteria for judging whether a piece of information is worth citing.

So, to summarize, doing GEO is not about simply performing a single action to get good results. Instead, it requires doing well in brand, content, and distribution system construction—these three major areas. These are the three misconceptions I've summarized: First, SEO is still the foundation; second, GEO might not allow you to quickly turn things around; third, GEO cannot be done well through a single breakthrough, it requires systematic execution.

The first point is that SEO is the foundation, I emphasize it again.

The second point is that large models have a soft spot for big brands and leading brands; the Matthew effect is very obvious, making it difficult to compete with big brands. Therefore, we should consider focusing on long-tail, niche scenarios to influence GEO's effectiveness.

The third point is that the information sources for large models currently provide answers based on a combination of pre-trained data and real-time retrieval, as mentioned earlier. The fourth point is that when you ask a large model a question, it often breaks the question down into multiple sub-questions to find answers within its own large model system, or it breaks them down into many questions to search for answers in search engines. It breaks these down into sub-questions and then searches in its database or performs real-time retrieval, finally compiling an answer. Therefore, you should design page content based on this mechanism of breaking down questions, considering what common FAQ questions might arise from a user's perspective when focusing on a topic, and include them on the page. If these questions you write are similar to the large model's breakdown logic, the probability of being cited will be greatly increased.

The fifth point is that different large models cite different information sources. For example, Google's Gemini prefers to cite content from its own ecosystem, such as YouTube and Reddit (which it has invested in), believing this content to be more authentic. Claude is more closed, tending to rely on its own high-quality training data sources. Therefore, for ChatGPT, we mainly look at what information sources ChatGPT primarily cites and optimize accordingly. For ChatGPT, tech media like Business Insider and TechRadar might be key areas to focus on.

The sixth point is that ChatGPT accounts for 80% of the entire market share, as mentioned earlier, so it should be a key focus.

Based on these 6 points of understanding, we then formulated a strategy, dividing the tasks into three main areas:

The first area is regular SEO, which involves doing well in content, backlinks, product user experience, and technical issues like website speed optimization. These are standard SEO actions.

The second area is to enhance brand influence. When considering how to boost brand influence, as mentioned earlier, the most important core indicator is brand keyword search volume. So, the strategy is how to increase brand keyword search volume. The methods listed include PR, influencer marketing (finding influencers to promote products; interested followers will typically search for the brand keyword to find the product, so this channel needs to be utilized). Affiliate marketing works similarly: when a product is recommended by many experts and publishers, who often have their own private communities or blogs as influencers, the brand gets massive exposure, and followers usually click links or search for the brand keyword to find the product. The fourth method is advertising, where more money spent generally leads to greater influence. So, in terms of enhancing brand power, these channels can be considered.

The third important direction is influencing data sources, divided into two aspects: The first is to become a data source cited by large models, meaning your official website and its content are cited by large models. This is very difficult, especially for small websites, as large models prefer large brand content because it's safer and less likely to be wrong. The second is to be included in content already cited by large models. That is, if a prompt keyword you're interested in cites 10 data sources, can you add your brand to these 10 data sources? If you can, the next time a large model cites them, it's very likely to recommend your brand because it's in that data source. So, we approach it from this angle.

Affiliate requires a long cycle of communication and relationship building, and ads require significant funding to raise brand awareness. So, we chose PR; issuing one PR piece can lead to 200-500 media websites reprinting it immediately, creating many brand mentions on the internet instantly. PR is a recommended channel. Influencer marketing is similar; paying influencers to post videos can quickly generate a lot of brand keyword searches. It's direct and fast.

Regarding influencing data sources, we didn't expect a new small website to be cited by large models because it lacked recognition. So, the focus was on how to get included in data sources already cited by large models.

我觉得现在要做好GEO，主要的几个点：

第一，产品是否达到了PMF。如果产品还没有达到PMF，我不建议开启GEO渠道。因为GEO的核心是长期品牌信号的积累，如果现在的方向、市场定位都没有找到，实际上会经常变。也就是说传递给互联网、传递给大模型的信号会经常变，做的产品到底是给marketing的人生成广告视频，还是给YouTube创作者创造能带来流量的视频，还是在做社媒视频的定位？如果经常这样变化，会让大模型不知道你到底在哪个方向，混淆之后不知道在什么场景下推荐你。所以要有明确的PMF信号，找到自己的定位之后再开启。

第二，是否在寻找第二增长曲线？也就是说之前已经把SEO渠道、广告渠道、红人渠道做得不错，想再寻找新渠道、找一些突破，那么GEO就值得选择布局。因为它是一个偏长期的渠道，前期已经通过前面的渠道验证了产品，布局这些长期渠道会增加品牌的壁垒。

第一个正好延续前面没讲完的话题，关于GEO最重要的认知问题。除了前面讲到的3点之外，还有6个点可以跟大家分享。这是所有做接下来策略制定、动作执行的指引，算是顶层设计的逻辑。

第一点是SEO是基础，再强调一遍。

第二点是大模型对大品牌、头部品牌情有独钟，头部效应很明显，很难去跟大品牌竞争。所以应该考虑做一些长尾、细分场景去影响GEO的效果。

第三点是大模型现在的信息源，给出来的答案是预训练的数据加上实时检索，前面也讲到了。基于这个特点，后面定的策略要根据这个来，想办法怎么样影响到预训练的数据，怎么样影响到实时检索的数据。

第四点是当向大模型提出一个问题时，大模型往往会把问题拆分成多个问题，在它自己的大模型系统里面找答案，或者说拆分成很多问题去搜索引擎里面找答案。举个例子，问"这个周末去深圳哪一个公园拍婚纱照比较合适"，它就会把问题拆解成：深圳这个周末的天气怎么样？深圳哪些公园的人流量分布怎么样？哪些公园是否允许带拍摄设备进去？拆成这些子问题再去库里面搜索或实时检索，最终汇总成一个答案。所以应该根据这种拆解问题的机制设计页面内容，考虑到聚焦一个主题时，从用户角度考虑应该有哪些常见的FAQ问题，可以写在页面上。如果写的这些问题和大模型拆解的逻辑相似，引用的概率就会被大大增加。

第五点，不同的大模型引用的信息源不一样。比如谷歌的Gemini比较喜欢引用自己生态下的内容，引用YouTube、Reddit（是它投资的）等平台的内容，认为这些内容比较真实。Claude比较封闭，更倾向于自己训练的高质量数据源。所以针对ChatGPT，主要看ChatGPT主要引用的信息源有哪些，针对性优化。对ChatGPT来说，像Business Insider、TechRadar这些科技媒体可能是要重点去做的。

第六点是前面讲到的ChatGPT占整个80%的市场份额，应该重点关注。

基于这6点认知，接下来制定策略，把要做的事情分了三大块：

第一块是常规SEO，要做好内容、外链、产品用户体验、技术端网站速度优化等技术端问题，这是SEO常规动作。

第二块要提升品牌影响力，想到能够提升品牌影响力的，前面讲到最重要的核心指标是品牌词搜索量，所以策略是要怎么样提高品牌词搜索量。列举出来的有PR、红人营销，找红人推广产品，红人粉丝对产品感兴趣，通常会搜索品牌词找到产品，所以要做这个渠道。Affiliate联盟营销也是一样道理，让产品被很多达人推荐、被很多publisher推荐，通常他们有自己的私域或自己做红人有博客，推荐出去品牌会被大量曝光，粉丝找产品时通常也是点链接或搜品牌词。第四个可以做广告投放，投的钱越多影响力肯定越大。所以在品牌力提升上面可以从这些渠道考虑。

第三个重要方向是影响数据源，分两个方向：第一个是成为大模型引用的数据源，即自己的官网、网站里面的内容被大模型引用，这个非常有难度，特别是小网站很难被大模型引用，因为大模型喜欢大品牌内容，引用大品牌内容更安全，基本上不会出错。第二个是加入到已经被大模型引用的内容里面去，即关注的prompt关键词引用了10条数据源，能不能把品牌加到这10条数据源里面去？如果加进去了，下一次大模型引用时很有可能就会推荐，因为在这个数据源里面。所以从这里去找手做。

做换脸项目时，因为是非常小投入的产品，不可能所有渠道都去做，没有很多预算，所以进行了筛选。基础SEO必须做。品牌力提升上面只做PR和红人营销，Affiliate和Ads不做，因为Affiliate要花很长周期沟通建联，Ads需要花很大资金才能把品牌知名度提起来。所以选择PR，发出去一篇PR会有200-500家媒体网站立即转载，瞬间能够在互联网上有很多品牌提及，PR是推荐的渠道。红人营销也差不多，花了钱红人发视频，马上就能够拿到很多品牌词搜索，比较直接、快速。

在影响数据源这边，不期望新的小网站能够被大模型引用，因为没有什么知名度，所以重点放在怎么样加入到大模型已经引用过的数据源里面。

这是当时定下来的策略。

具体怎么做好？首先说到四件事情：SEO、PR、红人营销和加入大模型引用的数据源。PR里面有一些，因为是在做GEO的尝试，所以整体目标是做GEO优化、拿到GEO流量，里面的一些做法可能会反常识。比如发新闻稿，很多时候发新闻稿的主题像SaaS产品升级大版本、接入DeepSeek、和谁联合做了什么事情、公司融资了等。但现在为了做GEO，发的新闻稿是针对目标想排名、想让大模型推荐的prompt关键词去做的PR稿。PR稿里面要反复提及品牌和想要推广的场景，比如想做Mac上面的换脸工具，产品有这样功能，就去发"在Mac上面做了一个应用，能够在本地实现换脸"这样的新闻稿，不会讲通用换脸效果多好、真实性可以导出等，而是针对"Mac换脸"这个关键词发PR，带上品牌名和反复出现场景提及。

发PR的时候要选稍微知名一点的PR平台，因为想要大量曝光。选小平台曝光力度可能不够，发一个PR出去可能只有50家、100家媒体转载，但发大平台可能有三五百家媒体转载，要的是大量提及，这是目标，所以要选大平台。

红人营销有点反常识的是，一般做红人营销都是看ROI的，花了1000美金或1万美金，希望这个月能够收回来12000美金回报，这样考核。但现在做GEO，目标是提升品牌词搜索量，没有考核ROI，是看品牌词搜索量作为KPI。所以做这个实验时，主要是找印度红人。为什么？因为印度红人相对便宜，能影响到的印度人比较多，在印度地区产品能够有大量曝光，能够带来很多的品牌词搜索找到产品。虽然不能贡献revenue，但能贡献品牌词的搜索量，这是反常识的点。

一些常规动作也会去做，找到合适的红人可以在视频下面编辑视频描述，把聊的评论置顶，YouTube视频评论可以做置顶等，红人营销这边通常也会去做的。

第三个是加入引用的数据源，这个动作是最关键的动作，做下来感觉也是最有效的。因为这些信息源被大模型引用，联系这些信息源问能不能把品牌加进去。如果10个都加进去，大模型后面被问到Mac端视频换脸时一定会推荐，因为在所有信息源里面，这是逻辑，看下来也是最有效的。

当然这个也是最花时间的。怎么做呢？当输入一个prompt在ChatGPT或其他模型里面，可以看到所有引用的信息源，引用的信息源有Reddit、Quora、YouTube、博客、科技媒体、PR等各种形态。所以要针对不同形态做不同动作。

如果发现有一个Reddit帖子作为信息源被大模型引用，就要找到这个帖子，去下面做评论，给评论点赞投票upvote，让评论顶上去。观察到如果评论在最下面，没有通过upvote顶上去，大模型是不会引用这条评论里面的内容的。应该是在节省内存和算力，只有排在前面的评论才是有价值的，后面的评论可能直接忽略掉。这是我观察到的，如果评论在下面基本上没用，这条评论是白做的。所以做Reddit评论时必须要求进前三，通常情况是第一位，只有在竞争非常激烈的post下面，前三也可以，因为太难了，可能要几十个upvote、几十条评论才能顶上去。

这是针对Reddit。Reddit还有一种情况，发现帖子被引用了想去评论已经不能评论了，因为已经归档archive。可以模仿这条帖子，再去发一个类似的帖子。因为这个帖子之前被引用过，发一个类似的帖子很有可能能够取代之前发的帖子，然后被大模型引用。当然要对帖子进行一些丰富，不能只是发一个帖子，还要维护帖子，给post做丰富的comments。这些comments要和话题、post相关，增强相关性，丰富post页面里面的内容，这些内容都是和主题相关的，才有可能被引用，不然也是一条垃圾post，不会被引用。

如果看到的是博客被引用，这种比较好操作，直接找到网站这篇博客，联系网站站长或管理员或运营相关人员，发邮件问能不能推荐品牌，品牌有什么优势。联系对方通常都是要钱的，如果这个关键词prompt是非常高价值的，就应该花这个预算。因为能出现在更多被引用的博客里面，被大模型推荐的概率就越高。

还可能发现像YouTube这样的信息源，可以做视频的评论、视频的描述，可以模仿视频做一个类似的视频，可以联系红人做一个已经发布的类似视频等动作。如果遇到网站引用的信息源是导航站点，可以去导航站点做AI工具的提交，通常这种也是要钱的。同理，遇到什么样的引用源，就应该想到对应的策略：遇到论坛或Reddit就去评论，有些小论坛就注册提交评论；遇到下载站点（做APP或插件的）就去下载站点提交；可能发现PR平台作为信息源，就应该去再发类似的PR。

Monica

听下来感觉把大模型当成一个比较心仪的想去追的人，到处刷脸刷存在感，在他喜欢的地方出现就对了。

Span

对，是的。实践下来这是最有效的。如果能够在，特别是看到之前公司的网站，在品类最好的软件品类关键词下面谷歌搜索结果前十里面都推荐了产品。所以当去大模型里面搜相关最好的品类产品时，不管用哪个大模型、用什么近义词，公司产品都会被推荐出来，有可能在第一位、第三位或第五位，但最重要的是很多时候都能够被推荐。这最重要，因为能够出现在推荐里面，就有机会被选择。

Monica

让大模型看到才有机会。博客站长要花多少钱，怎么样去衡量投入金额的价值？

Span

如果做过SEO的同学应该比较清楚，实际上就跟做SEO外链一样。一般会定一些不同的tier，有不同的价格范围。通常举一些例子：对方网站权威度，用Ahrefs的DR（Domain Rating，域名评分），DR在50左右，网站月访问流量在100K左右，像这种能接受的大概是300美金做一个链接。如果DR是70，月访问流量是100万以上，预算可能是500甚至700-800刀，跟做外链的判断逻辑一样，看对方网站质量。但如果非常想做这个信息源影响，可以比通常做SEO时高出一些溢价，因为非常想拿下这一条链接，影响到这个信息源。

好，那我们刚刚讲的就是有一个目标的关键词或prompt之后怎么做的，丢到大模型里面看引用的信息源，然后看每一个信息源能不能做一些什么动作。这里面有两个问题：第一个是怎么样拿到比较真实的用户输入的prompts；第二个是做这件事情会非常繁琐，要把每一个目标关键词prompt丢到每一个大模型里面去，收集每一个引用的信息源，收集下来可能几百条，非常繁琐，特别是当目标想做GEO的关键词prompt比较多的时候，有非常多工作要做。

这里推荐两个工具，自己用的。之前是用刚说的方式手动执行，后面用工具能够大幅提高效率。第一个是Ahrefs的Brand Radar，能够一次性找到所在行业、想排的所有相关真实用户prompts。当然真实打一个引号，不知道真不真实，但至少收集下来看起来比单纯关键词真人感要强一些，不再是"Best AI video generator"这样的关键词，而是确实有一些场景存在的"What's the best AI video generator for TikTok"这样的prompt，能够一次性拿到。用这样的prompt去做信息源分析会更加准确。

第二个工具叫Profound，能够一次性把几百个想要关注的prompt丢给它，作用是每天拿这些prompt在每一个大模型里面搜索，然后自动收集所有信息源。也就是说能够节省很大效率，一次性拿到所有信息源，然后只要去分类，按照刚说的Reddit怎么做、YouTube怎么做、博客怎么做、X怎么做，一类一类去操作。

整个执行思路就是这样子去做。整个项目做下来，从1月份到3月份实际上是在做产品，还有做一些基础SEO工作。4月份到6月份做了PR、YouTube、Reddit动作，五六月份之后基本上没有做太多推广动作，主要在做维护工作。

整体下来发布了三篇PR，在稍微知名一点的平台，像美通社、雅虎财经、Business Insider上面。YouTube视频做了16个，以印度这边的视频红人为主。Reddit因为效果比较明显、比较好，发的比较多，所有post和comments满足刚说的post是非常丰富的页面，comments都排到了前三位置。挑选出来的被引用的post，以及挑选出来想要影响到GEO的关键词和prompts。Quora做了20条，Quora比Reddit难做一点，更容易封号，操作难度大，做得比较少。博客插入做了三条链接，总共做了这些事情。结果是12月份去看的时候，从3月份开始品牌词搜索量一直在上涨，网站流量也在增加。看GEO这边的结果，到12月份是每个月有12K的大模型推荐流量，主要来自于ChatGPT。GEO流量数据在Profound数据看板里面看到，在同类竞品中是在第三位。

Monica

Quora有什么避坑的点？

Span

Quora和Reddit一起讲，其实Quora和Reddit很相似。Quora和Reddit都没有自己做，因为自己做很难，基本上一发就被封号，不管怎么样养号都挺难，自己养号也非常花时间。所以有一些tips，可以找Upwork上面的freelancer去做。为什么找他们做更好？因为他们专业做这个事情，可能有群控软件，养了很多号，已经养了很多年，对Reddit、Quora平台非常熟悉，知道怎么样去发，账号权重也比较高，相对能够比较容易安全地排在前面，给comments投票、做comments，都比自己做安全很多。

自己做主要是太花时间，直接找freelancer去做。很重要的一个tips是可以按照结果付费。也就是不管过程，只有发成功了、排进前三了才给钱，不然不给钱，也不用管是怎么做的。这是最省事的一个点，按结果付费。

Monica

这个是freelancer大部分可以接受的吗？

Span

这个是要每一个实际去聊，大部分聊下来是能够接受的。

观众

对GEO优化一轮后续维护的频率大概是什么样的？

Span

最好的是持续做，因为前面讲到GEO是一个持续做品牌建设的工作，所以最好是持续做。当然也可以按照SEO的方式定期维护。做SEO时会把一些工作分一些tier，比如拿博客更新来说，会把博客分层，不同层次。第一层是直接能带来订单转化的第一梯队内容，可能每个月要看一下排名有没有波动，有哪些新竞品排上去了、下降了，要观察、更新内容。第二梯队能带来注册，可能3到6个月才去看一次。第三梯队是支持性内容，带来转化流量都非常少，可能6个月或一年才去看一次。不同重要性，处理频次、对待态度也不一样。

观众

有什么办法知道ChatGPT的流量是怎么来的吗？比如用户输入的prompt。

Span

通过GA4就可以看到，只要接入了GA4，就能够看到每一个大模型来的流量。用户输入的prompt，自己网站上面Bing的Webmaster Tools是可以看到的，但数据比较少，因为只有Bing的数据，毕竟Bing只有谷歌1/10不到，所以Bing这边能够看到真实用户输入的prompts来到网站。谷歌这边现在还没有开放，所以最真实的目前只有Bing。但如果不是要求最真实的，可以看这个行业、品类的，用刚说的工具Ahrefs Brand Radar，和产品相关的用户搜索的prompts是什么。但它不能和网站关联，网站数据只有Bing后台可以看到。Ahrefs看到的是全行业、品类相关的真实prompts，当然真实也打个引号，不知道数据从哪里来。

观众

请问电商独立站现在的AI流量能占到多少？

Span

不管是电商独立站还是SaaS网站，实际上现在AI来的流量还非常少。从比例上面，观察到的一些网站，可能是SEO这边的1/20，或其他渠道的1/20。这也是前面讲的，GEO虽然非常火，但其实体量还是挺小的，1/20、1/30都很正常。

Monica

如果是多语言场景下的GEO优化，会不会有一些特殊的点？

Span

多语言的话，听说过一些特殊的点，但没有实践过，挺有意思的。有些品类竞争很激烈，英语市场很难，很难通过外部布局内容把GEO效果做好。但换一种思路，去做日文的Reddit内容、做韩语的，平台上面内容建设，这些语种竞争都非常小。这些语种也同时会被大模型拿去做训练，数据会进到大模型里面去。大模型会把这些信息做一些翻译，也就是说法语内容翻译成英文，有可能用户在搜英文prompt时，找不到太多信息，会把法语内容拿过来做推荐。做小众语种能够影响到英语搜索结果的。

Monica

最后请Span收个尾，如果只给大家一句关于GEO的运营建议的话，是什么？

Span

一句话的话，就是接下来包括自己也会是这样的策略：所有的运营动作都会围绕品牌词的提及来展开。也就是说有一个优先级，优先考虑那些能够带来品牌提及的渠道动作，Reddit、YouTube、红人以及联系科技媒体做宣发的动作都考虑这一点。如果不能带来品牌提及，这个营销动作优先级就会往后放。因为品牌提及是GEO时代的外链，现在看到的很多统计数据来看，品牌提及实际上是影响GEO效果的最重要的维度，比自己站内做一些东西的影响程度要高很多，外部信号比内部信号要高。
