The New Blue Ocean of AI Overseas Expansion

When talking about the AI market, people tend to divide it into tiers by default:

The U.S. ranks in the first echelon (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta); China takes the second echelon (DeepSeek, Tongyi Qianwen, Ernie Bot, Zhipu AI, Baichuan AI); Europe and India form the third echelon (Mistral, Infosys); while all other regions are merely regarded as application markets.

Central Asia is barely mentioned in most AI industry reports. With a population of 80 million, a GDP of 450 billion US dollars, numerous minority languages, relatively authoritarian governance models, and no established core clusters for AI talents or computing power, it truly stays out of the spotlight.

Yet this is exactly where massive opportunities lie.

The second half of the AI industry competition is no longer about large model research and development, but focuses on localized adaptation, vertical scenario implementation, multilingual capabilities, sovereign AI development and infrastructure overseas deployment. In these fields, Central Asia is a severely underestimated and fast-growing market.

Five Unique Advantages of AI Opportunities in Central Asia

  1. Optimal population structure for AI popularization

    Its median age ranges from 24 to 31 years old, internet penetration exceeds 80%, mobile payment is well-established and social media enjoys great popularity, making it one of the youngest and most digitally-savvy regions worldwide.

  2. Unique integration of languages, data and market demand

    Languages including Kazakh, Uzbek, Kyrgyz and Tajik are all low-resource minority languages with stable user bases, yet Western mainstream large models lack sufficient training data for them. Chinese AI models empowered with localized optimization perfectly fill this market gap.

  3. Strong governmental push for digital transformation

    Countries across the region have launched top-down digital strategies: Kazakhstan’s Digital Kazakhstan 2025, Uzbekistan’s Digital Uzbekistan 2030 and Turkmenistan’s Arkadag Smart City initiative. Local governments are investing heavily in AI-related construction.

  4. Higher acceptance of Chinese technologies over Western counterparts

    Central Asian governments prefer cost-effective, highly customizable services with comprehensive after-sales support, which perfectly aligns with the strengths of Chinese tech enterprises.

  5. Mature pre-built computing power infrastructure

    The crypto mining boom between 2021 and 2022 has laid solid foundations of power resources and data centers in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan, which can be smoothly transformed to serve AI computing needs.

Opportunity Map of Central Asia’s AI Industry in Four Core Dimensions

Infrastructure Layer: Computing Power, Power Supply & Data Centers

Kazakhstan: The Computing Power Hub of Central Asia

After China banned crypto mining in 2021, around 2 million mining machines were relocated to Kazakhstan, once contributing 18% of the global Bitcoin computing power. Later, power shortages and tightened regulations forced most mining farms to shut down.

Legacy Resources Left Behind

  • Over 1,500 megawatts of completed power supply configurations

  • Dozens of large-scale completed data center assets

  • Mature technical teams specializing in cooling, power distribution and daily operation

  • Rising governmental awareness of the strategic value of computing power infrastructure

Industry Transformation from 2023 to 2024

  • The government proposed the "Computing Power Corridor" initiative, encouraging traditional mining facilities to shift into AI computing centers, cloud service platforms and scientific computing bases.

  • Astana Hub launched a national-level AI supercomputer project in 2024 to build state-owned core AI computing platforms.

  • Active investment negotiations on AI computing power cooperation with China, Russia and the UAE.

  • KEGOC, Kazakhstan’s national power grid enterprise, signed differentiated electricity pricing agreements with data center operators.

Core Opportunities

  • Green computing power & cooling technology: Frigid winters in northern Kazakhstan enable natural cooling to lower data center PUE, bringing huge business potential for Chinese manufacturers like Inspur, Sugon, Huawei and Lenovo.

  • Power infrastructure construction: With an installed power capacity of about 25GW and limited room for new power load growth, Chinese PV and energy storage enterprises have broad market space.

  • Data center upgrading: Strong market demand for Tier II to Tier III facility renovation, as well as cold-hot aisle systems, server racks, UPS equipment and generator sets.

Uzbekistan: A Fast Follower in Digital Development

The Uzbek government rolled out the IT Park plan in 2023, aiming to cultivate 100,000 IT professionals and attract 10 billion US dollars in foreign IT investment by 2030.

Key Ongoing Projects

  • The core zone of Tashkent IT Park has been completed, accommodating thousands of tech enterprises.

  • Specialized data center parks are under construction with a planned capacity exceeding 200MW.

  • Established strategic cooperation with Infosys, Tata Group, Yandex and Huawei.

  • AI is listed as one of the six core development directions under the Digital Uzbekistan 2030 strategy.

Kyrgyzstan & Tajikistan: Hydropower-Driven Green Computing Power

Both countries boast some of the world’s lowest electricity prices at 0.02-0.03 US dollars per kWh, supported by abundant hydropower resources, which perfectly meets the global demand for eco-friendly AI computing power.

Kyrgyzstan legalized crypto asset mining by enacting the Virtual Asset Law in 2023, and many local data centers started transforming into AI computing service providers in 2024.

Upon the full operation of Tajikistan’s Rogun Hydropower Station, surplus power resources will turn the country into a core green energy supplier for Central Asian AI industries.

Turkmenistan: Closed yet Inseparable from AI Development

Despite its relatively closed market environment, large-scale data center construction is indispensable for Ashgabat’s smart city projects and the upcoming Arkadag City. Huawei and ZTE have undertaken most of the core infrastructure construction tasks.

Large Model & Basic Model Layer: Untapped Low-Resource Language Market

Central Asian Languages: Underserved Low-Resource Languages in the AI World

Excluding Russian, all official languages of the five Central Asian countries belong to low-resource languages, suffering from insufficient online corpus, training datasets and open-source model adaptation support:

  • Kazakh: 15 million native speakers, belonging to the Kipchak branch of Turkic languages

  • Uzbek: 35 million native speakers, belonging to the Karluk branch of Turkic languages

  • Kyrgyz: 4 million native speakers, belonging to the Kipchak branch of Turkic languages

  • Tajik: 9 million native speakers, closely related to Persian

  • Turkmen: 5 million native speakers, belonging to the Oghuz branch of Turkic languages

These languages have a combined user base of around 70 million native speakers, exceeding that of many European languages. However, mainstream large models including GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, LLaMA, Qwen and DeepSeek only offer basic translation and question-answering functions for them, with unsatisfying accuracy, fluency and cultural comprehension capability compared with English and Chinese.

Local Independent Large Model Exploration in Central Asia

Kazakhstan

  • Nazarbayev University jointly launched the prototype KazakhGPT with national scientific research institutions in 2023.

  • Kazakhstan Telecom partnered with the national AI center to release the multilingual translation model Tildes supporting Kazakh, Russian and English in 2024.

  • It is exploring sovereign AI cooperation with top overseas AI enterprises to deploy external advanced models while storing all local data domestically.

Uzbekistan

  • Launched the Uzbek language dedicated model UzLang prototype in 2024.

  • Tashkent University built joint AI laboratories with Tsinghua University and Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

  • Local AI startups are being incubated steadily in Tashkent IT Park.

Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Turkmenistan mainly rely on localized adaptation of Yandex models and Chinese AI models, lacking independent large model R&D capabilities.

Golden Opportunities for Chinese AI Models in Central Asia

Central Asian markets have strong demand for cost-effective, localized and service-oriented AI models, which is exactly the core advantage of Chinese open-source large models.

DeepSeek

  • DeepSeek-R1 has gained widespread popularity among Central Asian developer communities since its release in 2024.

  • Numerous enterprises in Russia and Kazakhstan have built localized application products based on DeepSeek.

  • Its open-source feature, low cost and powerful reasoning capability perfectly fit the budget constraints of local government and enterprise clients.

Qwen (Tongyi Qianwen)

  • Alibaba Cloud has deployed server nodes in Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan.

  • Qwen series models deliver outstanding performance in Russian and Turkic language adaptation.

  • In-depth cooperation with local cloud service providers can greatly cut operational costs.

GLM (Zhipu AI)

  • ChatGLM shows excellent adaptability to Russian and various Central Asian languages.

  • Enterprise-level customized AI products are being steadily promoted across Central Asia.

Other mainstream Chinese models including Baichuan AI, Kimi and Doubao still have limited local penetration, yet their market entry channels are gradually opening up alongside the business expansion of Chinese internet giants in the region.

Practical Localization Implementation Paths

  1. Fine-tune Chinese open-source large models with local language corpus to launch exclusive Central Asian version models.

  2. Deploy AI models on local servers or regional cloud platforms to ensure all user data stays within national borders.

  3. Develop industry-specific lightweight small models for government affairs, education, agriculture and medical industries.

  4. Develop voice interaction and real-time translation models to meet booming cross-border communication demands.

  5. Deliver all-round national-level AI platform solutions in cooperation with local governments to support sovereign AI construction.

Application Layer: In-Demand Landing Scenarios

Government Affairs & Digital Governance

Central Asian countries are vigorously promoting digital government upgrading, with mature national-level platforms widely in use. Main AI application directions:

  • Multilingual 24/7 intelligent government customer service

  • Intelligent automatic processing of official documents and archives

  • Facial recognition, living body detection and digital identity authentication

  • Intelligent risk control for tax, social security and customs supervision

  • AI-enabled smart transportation, urban security and environmental monitoring

Fintech & Digital Payment

The fintech industry is booming in Central Asia, with leading local platforms emerging rapidly. Main AI application directions:

  • Intelligent risk assessment and anti-fraud detection for credit and transactions

  • Built-in AI dialogue assistants for financial mobile applications

  • Personalized financial product recommendation systems

  • AI credit scoring based on diversified user behavior data

  • Automated KYC review and anti-money laundering compliance procedures

Agricultural AI

Central Asia is a major agricultural region rich in cotton, wheat and fruits yet lagging behind in modern farming technology. Main AI application directions:

  • Precision agriculture management via satellite remote sensing and AI crop growth analysis

  • Intelligent irrigation scheduling to optimize water resource allocation

  • Mobile terminal-based crop disease and pest identification

  • Intelligent crop yield prediction to support bulk agricultural product trade

  • AI-powered drone intelligent spraying technology promotion

Educational AI

Severe uneven distribution of educational resources makes AI education an effective way to bridge regional gaps. Main AI application directions:

  • Intelligent localized translation of high-quality teaching materials

  • AI online tutoring services for students in remote areas

  • AI Chinese teaching robots catering to surging local Chinese learning demands

  • Vocational skill training covering IT, foreign languages and engineering courses

Mining & Energy Industry AI

As resource-rich regions, Central Asian countries are accelerating industrial digital transformation. Main AI application directions:

  • UAV visual inspection for oil-gas pipelines and mining safety monitoring

  • Equipment failure early warning and predictive maintenance

  • Digital twin modeling for mines, oil fields and power stations

  • AI-assisted geological exploration and resource analysis

  • Intelligent power grid dispatching and energy storage optimization

E-commerce & Retail AI

The cross-border e-commerce market maintains explosive growth momentum. Main AI application directions:

  • Locally optimized commodity recommendation algorithms

  • One-click multilingual commodity information translation for cross-border platforms

  • Intelligent multilingual after-sales customer service system

  • AI-based market demand forecasting and supply chain path optimization

Medical AI

Serious shortage of medical resources highlights the practical value of medical AI. Main application directions:

  • AI-assisted intelligent diagnosis of medical imaging reports

  • Remote expert consultation and telemedicine service support

  • Intelligent chronic disease daily health management system

  • Full-cycle pharmaceutical product traceability and anti-counterfeiting management

  • Intelligent translation of international medical academic materials

Short Video & AIGC Content Creation

Short video platforms are extremely popular among local young people, bringing huge opportunities for AIGC. Main application directions:

  • AI image generation tools for local content creators

  • One-click automatic short video editing and production

  • Intelligent copywriting for localized social media marketing

  • Virtual digital human live streaming and goods promotion

Overseas Expansion Strategies for Chinese AI Enterprises

Strategy 1: Joint Expansion with Established Chinese Enterprises (B2B2B Mode)

Take advantage of the mature local layout of Chinese giants including CNPC, Huawei and BYD. Provide customized AI industry tools for their smart mine, intelligent factory and smart logistics projects to achieve rapid local market penetration.

Strategy 2: Establish Partnerships with Local Leading Digital Enterprises

Deepen cooperation with mainstream local platforms such as Kaspi in Kazakhstan and Uzum in Uzbekistan. Position your enterprise as a professional AI technology capability supplier instead of a direct end-product competitor.

Strategy 3: Deliver All-Round Sovereign AI Solutions for Governments

Fully cater to local governments’ demand for sovereign AI that balances advanced technological capabilities and domestic data storage requirements. Provide integrated solutions covering models, computing power, data governance and localized operation services. This mature business model proven in the Middle East is highly replicable in Central Asia.

Strategy 4: Low-Threshold AI Assistant SaaS Layout

Start market entry with vertical-scenario AI office assistants covering contract sorting, multilingual translation and educational auxiliary tools. Adopt low-cost monthly subscription pricing to quickly capture small and medium-sized enterprise user groups, and embed AI functions into overseas versions of mainstream office collaboration platforms.

Strategy 5: Strategic Investment & Local Merger and Acquisition

Local emerging AI startups are still at low valuation stages. Chinese AI enterprises can accelerate localized team building and market resource integration through equity investment and merger and acquisition.

Potential Risks & Challenges

Data Sovereignty and Compliance Risks

Central Asian countries have formulated strict data localization laws, requiring all local citizen information to be stored in domestic servers. Overseas AI enterprises must build local data operation centers and strictly abide by regional data supervision regulations.

Complex Geopolitical Competition

The U.S., Russia and China are all competing for the Central Asian AI market. Western tech giants rely on cloud service cooperation to seize market share, while Russian enterprises gain advantages via linguistic and geopolitical proximity. Chinese enterprises need to maintain steady development amid intricate geopolitical situations. In addition, U.S. chip export control policies may bring new constraints to local AI hardware deployment.

Linguistic and Cultural Barriers

Diversified local languages increase the full-scale layout difficulty for overseas enterprises. Meanwhile, local Islamic cultural traditions put forward special requirements on AI content generation and ethical norms, requiring professional local consulting teams for cultural adaptation.

Slow Commercialization Progress

The overall Central Asian AI market is still in the initial development stage with limited single-project profit margins. Government procurement projects feature long approval cycles and delayed payment settlement. Enterprises need to make long-term sustainable operation plans instead of pursuing short-term high profits.

Severe Talent Shortage

Local professional AI talent reserves are insufficient with imperfect domestic training systems. High costs for dispatching Chinese technical personnel restrict team expansion, while the inflow of Russian IT talents brings both human resource opportunities and intensified industry competition.

Ten-Year Outlook: Central Asia AI Development Blueprint 2026-2036

2026-2027: Infrastructure Construction Stage

Large-scale data center construction will be completed one after another; national core AI computing power platforms will be officially launched; the sovereign AI development concept will gain universal recognition; Chinese computing power infrastructure manufacturers will dominate the regional market.

2028-2031: Comprehensive Application Outbreak Stage

AI technologies will be widely applied in government affairs, finance, agriculture and education fields; 3 to 5 local AI unicorns with a valuation exceeding 1 billion US dollars are expected to emerge; Central Asia will become a core core hinterland for Chinese AI global expansion and form industrial linkage with South Asia, the Middle East and Southeast Asia.

2032-2036: Mature Ecological Development Stage

An independent and complete local AI industrial ecosystem will take shape; self-developed local large models will reach international leading levels in Turkic and Persian language fields; in-depth industrial integration between China and Central Asia will be realized, and core AI industrial clusters will rise in Tashkent and Astana; AI-related industries are expected to contribute over 10% to local total GDP.

Practical Suggestions for Overseas Enterprises & Investment Institutions

Suggestions for Overseas Enterprises

  • Central Asia is not a market for quick huge profits, but a strategic highland for long-term ecological layout.

  • Take Central Asia as a training base to accumulate practical experience for expanding into emerging global southern markets.

  • Regulate it as a key springboard to radiate business layout across the Russian-speaking region, the Middle East and South Asia.

Suggestions for Investment Institutions

  • The Central Asian AI track is severely undervalued with enormous development potential.

  • The period from 2026 to 2028 is the best window for market entry and strategic layout.

  • Focus on three core investment directions: basic computing power construction, local minority language large models and vertical industry AI SaaS services.

  • The greatest market opportunities lie in the integrated development of AI and traditional local industries, rather than pure AI technology research and development.

The AI Era Belongs to Central Asia

A century ago, this land served as the core hub of the ancient Silk Road.

Fifty years ago, it was built into an important raw material supply base for cotton, oil and gas resources.

Thirty years ago, these independent countries started exploring their own development paths with no mature experience to follow.

What role will it play in the future?

It is poised to become an important core battlefield in the global AI competition. Boasting a young demographic structure, strong public digital acceptance, powerful governmental driving force and superior geographical location, Central Asia holds great potential to become an indispensable part of the third pole of global AI development.

Value-added strategic layouts are always completed in overlooked emerging markets rather than crowded mainstream tracks.

AI出海的新蓝海

谈及AI市场，大家会习惯性地分层：美国是第一梯队（OpenAI、Google、Anthropic、Meta）、中国是第二梯队（DeepSeek、通义千问、文心一言、智谱、百川）、欧洲和印度是第三梯队（Mistral、Infosys）、其他国家都被视为"应用市场"。

中亚在绝大多数AI报告里甚至没有出场——8000万人口、4500亿美元GDP、多种小语种、政府治理偏威权、不是传统的AI人才或算力中心。看起来确实不在聚光灯下。

但这恰恰是机会所在。

因为AI产业的下半场不是模型竞争，而是本地化应用、垂直场景落地、多语言能力、主权AI、基础设施出海——在这些维度上，中亚是一个被严重低估的、蓬勃发展的市场。

中亚AI机会的五个独特性

1.人口结构最适合AI普及：中位年龄24-31岁、互联网渗透率80%+、移动支付成熟、社交媒体活跃——这是全球最年轻、最数字化的地区之一。

2.语言—数据—市场的独特组合：哈萨克语、乌兹别克语、吉尔吉斯语、塔吉克语都是"小语种"但有稳定受众，英美大模型训练不足，"中国模型+本地化"恰好是空白。

3.政府强推数字化转型：哈萨克"数字哈萨克2025"、乌兹别克"数字乌兹别克2030"、土库曼"Arkadag智慧城市"——各国政府都在大力投资AI。

4.中国技术比西方技术更容易被采纳：中亚政府偏好成本低、定制强、服务好的合作伙伴，中国企业恰合其意。

5.算力基础设施沉淀：2021-2022年加密浪潮在哈萨克、吉尔吉斯沉淀了大量电力和数据中心资源，AI时代这些基础设施可以转型。

接下来，我们从四个层面来看看中亚AI产业的机会地图。

基础设施层：算力、电力、数据中心

哈萨克斯坦：中亚算力首都

2021-2022年中国禁止加密挖矿后，约200万台矿机迁入哈萨克斯坦，一度让哈萨克占全球比特币算力的18%。但随后电力短缺、政府紧缩监管，大量矿场关闭。

遗产：

•累计超过1500兆瓦的电力配置已完成；

•十几个大型数据中心资产已建成；

•冷却、供配电、运维等技术团队已沉淀；

•政府认识到算力基础设施的战略价值。

2023-2024年的转型：

•政府推出"算力走廊"概念——鼓励数据中心从加密挖矿向AI算力中心、云服务、科学计算转型；

•Astana Hub 2024年宣布建设"AI超级计算机"项目，目标是部署国家级AI算力平台；

•与中国、俄罗斯、阿联酋多方洽谈AI算力投资；

•哈萨克电网公司（KEGOC）与数据中心运营商签署差异化电价协议。

机会：

•冷却技术与绿色算力：哈萨克北部冬季严寒，天然冷却降低数据中心PUE（能源使用效率），中国数据中心技术厂商（浪潮、曙光、华为、联想等）有大机会；

•电力基础设施：哈萨克总装机容量约25GW，但新增负荷受限，中国光伏和储能企业（隆基、阳光电源、宁德时代、远景等）有空间；

•数据中心建设：从Tier II到Tier III的升级，冷热通道、机架、UPS、发电机组等设备需求巨大。

乌兹别克斯坦：迅速追赶者

乌兹别克政府2023年推出"IT Park"计划，目标到2030年培养10万IT专业人员、吸引100亿美元外资IT投资。

具体项目：

•塔什干IT Park核心区建成，容纳数千家IT企业；

•新设"Data Center"园区，规划容量超过200MW；

•与印度Infosys、Tata、俄罗斯Yandex、中国华为签约合作；

•推出"Digital Uzbekistan 2030"，把AI列为6大核心方向之一。

吉尔吉斯斯坦和塔吉克斯坦：水电驱动的绿色算力

两国电力价格极低（每度约0.02-0.03美元，世界最低之一），主要来自水电——这正是全球AI算力绿色化所需要的。

吉尔吉斯斯坦2023年通过《虚拟资产法》，加密货币挖矿合法化；2024年有数据中心向AI计算转型。

塔吉克斯坦罗贡水电站（Rogun HPP）建成后，电力过剩，可能成为中亚AI算力的"绿色电池"。

土库曼斯坦：封闭但不缺席

土库曼虽封闭，但阿什哈巴德"智慧城市"项目和未来"Arkadag城"都要求配套大规模数据中心。华为、中兴承担了主要建设任务。

大模型与基础模型层：多语言空白地带

中亚语言：AI世界的"低资源语言"

中亚五国的官方语言中，除俄语外，都是"低资源语言"——互联网语料、训练数据、开源模型支持都很有限：

•哈萨克语（约1500万母语者）：突厥语系钦察语支；

•乌兹别克语（约3500万母语者）：突厥语系卡尔鲁克语支；

•吉尔吉斯语（约400万母语者）：突厥语系钦察语支；

•塔吉克语（约900万母语者）：伊朗语系，与波斯语接近；

•土库曼语（约500万母语者）：突厥语系乌古斯语支。

加起来约7000万母语使用者，比许多欧洲国家语言人口还多。但主流大模型（GPT-4、Claude、Gemini、LLaMA、Qwen、DeepSeek）对这些语言的支持参差不齐——基本的翻译和问答能做，但准确度、流畅度、文化理解都远不及英语或中文。

中亚自身的大模型探索

哈萨克斯坦：

•2023年，纳扎尔巴耶夫大学联合科学研究院发布"KazakhGPT"原型；

•2024年，哈萨克电信与国家AI中心合作推出"Tildes"多语言翻译模型，支持哈、俄、英；

•哈萨克斯坦还在探索与OpenAI、Anthropic、Google建立"主权AI"合作——利用外部模型能力但保存在本地数据中心。

乌兹别克斯坦：

•2024年发布"UzLang"——针对乌兹别克语的语言模型原型；

•2024年，塔什干大学与清华大学、上海交大建立联合AI实验室；

•塔什干IT Park正在孵化本土AI初创公司。

吉尔吉斯、塔吉克、土库曼：主要依赖俄罗斯Yandex和中国模型的本地化改造，自研能力有限。

中国模型的中亚机会

这是中国AI模型企业的黄金机会——中亚市场对"高性价比 + 本地化 + 服务好"的模型有强烈需求，而中国开源大模型正好匹配这一需求：

DeepSeek：

•2024年DeepSeek-R1发布后，在中亚开发者社区获得广泛关注；

•俄罗斯、哈萨克多家企业基于DeepSeek构建本地化应用；

•低成本、开源、推理能力强，尤其适合预算有限的中亚政企客户。

通义千问（Qwen）：

•阿里云在哈萨克和乌兹别克有服务器节点；

•Qwen系列模型在俄语、突厥语支持上表现不错；

•与本地云服务商合作可以大规模降本。

智谱清言（GLM）：

•ChatGLM系列模型对俄语和中亚多语言适应性良好；

•面向政企客户的企业级产品正在拓展中亚市场。

百川、月之暗面（Kimi）、豆包（字节）：

•目前中亚渗透有限，但随着中资背景互联网公司（字节、腾讯、阿里）在中亚布局深入，这些模型的可能进入渠道正在打开。

落地路径

1. 本地化微调：用中亚语言语料对中国开源大模型进行微调，形成"中亚版本"；

2. 边缘部署：把模型部署到中亚本地服务器或运营商云，数据不出境；

3. 垂直行业：政务、教育、农业、医疗的专用小模型；

4. 语音+翻译：中亚国家跨境沟通需求旺盛，语音翻译大模型是刚需；

5. 主权AI服务：与中亚政府合作，提供"国家级AI平台"整体解决方案。

应用层：具体落地场景盘点

政务与数字政府

中亚国家普遍追求"数字政府"现代化——哈萨克eGov、乌兹别克MyID、吉尔吉斯TundukID都是国家级数字政务平台。AI应用空间：

•智能客服：7×24小时多语言政务咨询；

•文档智能：合同、公文、档案的自动化处理；

•数字身份：人脸识别、活体检测、多因素认证；

•反欺诈：税务、社保、海关场景的智能风控；

•城市治理：智慧交通、安全城市、环境监测。

金融科技与支付

中亚金融科技发展迅速，哈萨克Kaspi、乌兹别克Click和Uzum、吉尔吉斯O!Money都是头部玩家。AI应用空间：

•风控与反欺诈：信贷风险、交易异常检测；

•智能客服：移动APP里的AI对话助手；

•个性化推荐：金融产品、投资建议；

•信贷决策：替代数据（手机使用、社交、消费）的AI信用评分；

•合规自动化：KYC、反洗钱流程自动化。

农业AI

中亚是农业大国（棉花、小麦、水果），但农业技术水平整体落后。AI应用空间：

•精准农业：卫星遥感+AI分析，监测作物生长；

•水资源管理：阿姆河、锡尔河流域智能灌溉（咸海危机的核心问题）；

•病虫害识别：手机APP拍照即可识别；

•产量预测：为棉花、小麦贸易提供决策支持；

•无人机喷洒：已在哈萨克、乌兹别克试点。

教育AI

中亚教育体系资源分布极不均衡，城乡差距大，AI教育是补齐短板的重要路径：

•多语言教材翻译：把优质教材本地化；

•在线辅导：AI家教助力乡村学生；

•中文教学：中亚中文学习者激增，AI中文老师是刚需；

•职业培训：IT、英语、工程师技能培训。

矿业与能源AI

中亚资源大国的"数字化转型"——石油、天然气、矿山、电力：

•智能巡检：无人机+AI图像识别，油气管道、矿山安全监测；

•预测性维护：设备故障预警；

•数字孪生：矿山、油田、发电站的数字化模型；

•勘探优化：AI辅助地质分析；

•能源调度：智能电网与储能优化。

电商与零售AI

中亚电商市场爆发式增长，AI应用空间：

•推荐算法：本地化推荐引擎；

•商品翻译与本地化：Temu、AliExpress等中国电商平台的多语言需求；

•智能客服：俄语、哈萨克语、乌兹别克语自动客服；

•供应链优化：需求预测、库存管理、物流路径。

医疗AI

中亚医疗资源短缺，农村医疗基本靠"一位医生+几个护士"。AI应用：

•影像诊断：X光、CT的AI辅助判读；

•远程医疗：专家远程诊疗；

•慢病管理：糖尿病、高血压的AI管理；

•药品供应链：防假冒、溯源；

•医学翻译：国际医学文献本地化。

短视频与内容生成

TikTok、Instagram、YouTube在中亚年轻人中极其活跃。AI生成内容（AIGC）是新兴机会：

•图片生成：给内容创作者提供AI工具；

•视频剪辑：自动生成短视频；

•文案生成：本地语言的营销文案；

•虚拟主播：数字人直播带货。

中国AI企业出海中亚的路径建议

路径1：跟着中国大企业一起出海（B2B2B）

•中石油、华为、比亚迪等已经扎根中亚的大企业，是AI企业最可靠的"母基金"式合作伙伴；

•为他们提供AI工具（智慧矿山、智能工厂、智慧物流），可以快速完成在中亚的业务落地。

路径2：与中亚本地大型数字企业合作

•哈萨克Kaspi、乌兹别克Uzum、吉尔吉斯O!都在寻找AI能力合作伙伴；

•中国大模型公司可以把自己定位为"AI能力供应商"，而不是"终端APP竞争者"。

路径3：直接面向政府的"主权AI"方案

•哈萨克、乌兹别克都在探索"主权AI"概念——既要先进模型能力，又要数据不出境；

•中国公司可以提供"打包解决方案"：模型+算力+数据治理+本地化服务；

•这个模式在一些国家（如沙特、阿联酋）已经被阿里云、腾讯云、华为云复制，中亚是下一站。

路径4：小步快跑的Copilot路径

•从垂直场景的"AI助手"切入（HR助手、合同助手、翻译助手、教师助手）；

•以SaaS订阅模式，月费几十美元，低门槛进入中亚中小企业；

•飞书、Lark、钉钉的海外版本可以携带AI能力进入。

路径5：投资和并购

•中亚本土AI初创公司正在崛起，估值仍低；

•中国AI公司可以通过投资、并购本地团队，加速本地化。

风险与挑战

数据主权与合规

中亚国家对数据跨境流动越来越敏感。哈萨克斯坦、乌兹别克斯坦都已出台数据本地化法律，要求本国公民数据必须存储在境内。中国AI企业要做好本地数据中心和合规运营。

地缘政治

中美俄三方都在争夺中亚AI市场。美国OpenAI、Google、Microsoft通过云合作进入；俄罗斯Yandex、Sber基于语言和政治优势切入；中国企业需要在地缘敏感时期格外谨慎。

2023年美国出台的《芯片法案》对中亚的AI芯片出口管制可能成为新变量。

语言与文化

•中亚语言多元，中国AI企业难以独自覆盖所有语言；

•伊斯兰文化对AI伦理、内容生成有特定要求（避免冒犯、尊重宗教图像限制）；

•需要本地合作伙伴或顾问团队。

商业化节奏

•中亚市场规模和发展相对初期，单一项目回报有限；

•政府项目周期长、付款慢；

•需要做好"5年不赚钱"的准备。

人才

•中亚AI人才稀缺，本土培训体系不足；

•中资企业派遣成本高；

•俄罗斯IT人才涌入带来机会但也加剧竞争。

十年展望：2026-2036的中亚AI地图

2026-2027：基础设施期

•数据中心大规模建设；

•国家级AI算力平台上线；

•主权AI概念普及；

•中国算力基础设施厂商主导。

2028-2031：应用爆发期

•政务、金融、农业、教育AI大规模普及；

•本土AI独角兽涌现（可能出现3-5家估值10亿美元+的AI公司）；

•中亚成为中国AI出海的重要腹地；

•与印度、中东、东南亚形成AI产业链联动。

2032-2036：生态成熟期

•中亚形成独立的AI技术与应用生态；

•本土大模型在突厥语系、波斯语系达到国际水平；

•中亚与中国AI产业深度融合，形成"中亚硅谷"（最可能在塔什干或阿斯塔纳）；

•AI对GDP贡献率可能超过10%。

给出海企业和投资机构的一些建议

给出海企业

•中亚不是"赚大钱"的市场，是"建生态"的市场；

•把中亚作为出海全球南方的"练兵场"；

•用中亚作为俄语区、中东、南亚AI市场的"跳板"。

给投资机构

•中亚AI是被低估的赛道；

•2026-2028年是最佳入场窗口；

•关注算力、本地语言模型、垂直SaaS三条主线；

•最大的机会不在"纯AI"公司，而在"AI+传统产业"的结合点。

中亚的AI世纪

100年前，这里是丝绸之路的心脏。

50年前，这里是苏联的棉花工厂和油气基地。

30年前，这里是独立的、摸着石头过河的"斯坦国家"。

今天，这里正在成为什么？

也许是下一个AI世界的重要战场。人口年轻、数字化热情高、政府推动力强、地缘位置独特——中亚有成为全球AI"第三极"重要组成部分的潜力。

真正重要的布局，从来不是在热闹处，而是在被忽视的角落。
