OpenAI’s sales team once faced a major headache: its data coverage for potential customers stood at only 40%. Missing email addresses, incomplete company profiles, outdated contact roles — a classic B2B sales nightmare.

After adopting Clay, data coverage jumped to over 80%.

It isn’t just OpenAI. Star companies including Anthropic, Canva, and Ramp all rely on this same tool for sales automation. Called Clay, it looks like Excel but functions as an operating system for sales workflows.

Consider its funding trajectory: valued at $1.5 billion in May 2025, it doubled to $3.1 billion just three months later. The round was led by CapitalG, the growth equity arm of Google’s parent company Alphabet.

How did a “spreadsheet tool for sales teams” become a bidding war target for top Silicon Valley venture capitalists?

Product Breakdown: This Isn’t a CRM — It’s an Operating System for Sales Data

Clay is not what you might think: it is not a CRM like Salesforce, nor a sales execution tool like Apollo.

What is Clay? A data middle office that integrates 150+ data sources, AI research capabilities, and workflow automation into an Excel-like interface.

Architecture Breakdown

Layer 1: Data Aggregation Layer

Clay integrates with 150+ data providers:

  • Traditional databases: ZoomInfo, Apollo, Clearbit, Lusha

  • Intent signals: 6sense, Koala (monitors website visits, role changes, funding announcements)

  • Proprietary user data: supports integration via custom API keys

Core Innovation: Waterfall Enrichment

The traditional approach is to subscribe to ZoomInfo and give up if data is missing. Clay’s logic is different:

If Apollo fails to find an email → automatically try Hunter → then RocketReach → until the data is found.

Layer 2: AI Processing Layer (Core Competitive Advantage)

Claygent — Clay’s AI research agent — is essentially a programmable GPT-4 web crawler.

Give it a prompt such as “Is this company SOC II certified?” and it will:

  1. Automatically search relevant web pages

  2. Access the company’s official website / security page

  3. Extract key information

  4. Return structured answers

Real-world usage metrics:

  • 30% of Clay users use Claygent daily

  • 500,000 AI research tasks executed every day

  • Equivalent to a 24/7 research assistant for every sales team

Typical use cases: determining if a prospect is a B2B company, scraping recent funding news, analyzing competitor tech stacks, and extracting company pain points from job descriptions.

Layer 3: Workflow Automation Layer

Clay’s interface resembles Excel, but its backend is a no-code programming environment.

Example logic:

IF company size > 500 employees

→ pull data from ZoomInfo (premium but accurate)

ELSE

→ pull data from Apollo (cost-effective)

It can also call any HTTP API, connect to internal data sources, and support Webhook triggers: when new leads enter the CRM → Clay enrichment is automatically activated.

Credit-Based Pricing + PLG Growth = Why Capital Is Racing to Invest

Clay does not charge per seat; it bills based on consumed data credits.

Why not seat-based pricing?

  • ✅ Unlimited users → reduces friction for team-wide adoption

  • ✅ Pay-as-you-go → revenue directly tied to user value

  • ✅ Rollover credits → reduces user anxiety about “wasting” subscriptions

This is a classic PLG (Product-Led Growth) strategy: letting users grow organically instead of relying on sales push.

Why Did Google and Sequoia Invest at a $3.1B Valuation?

Investment Highlights

  1. Backing from star customers

    OpenAI, Anthropic, Canva, and Ramp all use Clay. These companies have extremely high tool standards, and their choice serves as a strong vote of confidence.

  2. Integration barrier of 150+ data sources

    Connecting 150+ APIs is not technically difficult, but maintaining them is. Each API has different formats, authentication rules, and rate limits, requiring ongoing engineering investment.

  3. Technical moat of Claygent

    Beyond calling OpenAI’s API, Clay has built unique expertise in prompt engineering and web crawling — something competitors cannot easily replicate in the short term.

  4. Booming GTM automation sector

    GTM (Go-To-Market) automation is the hottest SaaS category of 2025. Why? AI has turned “data-driven sales” from a slogan into reality.

  5. Platform-level upside

    Clay has already launched a native Sequencer (email automation). It may eventually build its own CRM and become a full-stack GTM platform.

Market Signal: Valuation Doubles in 3 Months

  • May 2025: Clay valued at $1.5B (led by Sequoia)

  • August 2025: Clay valued at $3.1B (led by CapitalG)

Behind this surge:

  • The “iPhone moment” has arrived for AI applications

  • A paradigm shift from “AI capabilities” (large model APIs) to “AI workflows” (integrated toolchains)

  • Capital’s verdict: data middle offices > single-point tools

Localization Opportunities: Why Directly Copying Clay Won’t Work in China

Key Barriers

  1. Lack of comparable data sources

  • LinkedIn is unavailable in mainland China

  • ZoomInfo / Apollo offer weak coverage of Chinese enterprise data

  • Requires integration of local providers: Qichacha, Tianyancha, Maimai, etc.

  1. Lower B2B maturity

  • China’s B2B sales rely more on relationship-building than data-driven tactics

  • Sales Ops roles remain underdeveloped in Chinese enterprises

  • Low willingness to pay an annual fee of $1,800 (≈ ¥13,000)

  1. Compliance risks

  • Automated web scraping may violate China’s Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL)

  • Restrictions on the use of phone and email data

Viable Entry Points

Direction 1: Vertical Industry Clay

  • Target sectors like pharmaceutical / financial SaaS (with more standardized B2B processes)

  • Integrate local data: Qichacha corporate info + Maimai professional updates + 36Kr funding data

  • Annual pricing: ¥5,000–8,000 (roughly one-third of the international version)

Direction 2: Services for Chinese Global Businesses

  • Ride the wave of Chinese SaaS going global (fueled by TikTok, Shein, etc.)

  • Help Chinese companies enrich overseas customer data

  • A reverse Clay model: connecting global data sources while serving Chinese teams

Direction 3: China-Style Private Domain Tool

  • WeCom (WeChat Work) version of Clay: automated tagging, tiered operations, content push

  • AI-powered personalized messaging (similar to Claygent)

  • Adapted to China’s private domain traffic ecosystem

Takeaways for Entrepreneurs

  • Opportunity in “middle office thinking”: integrating fragmented tools > reinventing the wheel

    Clay does not own its own database but unifies 150+ external sources — a textbook middle office strategy. For startups, instead of always building products from zero to one, integrating existing tools and lowering adoption barriers is often a faster path to PMF (Product-Market Fit).

  • PLG + credit-based pricing = standard SaaS model in 2025

    Per-seat licensing is outdated. Unlimited users + pay-as-you-go align with the AI era. If you’re building SaaS, consider “get users on board first, monetize through usage later.”

  • AI Agents are not hype — they cut labor costs

    Claygent runs 500,000 research tasks daily. This is not just “AI assistance” but “AI replacement” for repetitive work in junior research roles. Avoid superficial “AI-wrapped” products. Truly valuable AI applications let companies hire fewer people and eliminate entire workflows.

Conclusion

Clay is neither a “data company” (it uses third-party data) nor a pure “AI company” (it relies on OpenAI’s API). At its core, it is an operating system for sales workflows.

It proves one thing: in the AI era, opportunities lie not only in large foundation models but also in the application layer — embedding AI capabilities into real-world workflows.

For entrepreneurs, Clay points to a clear path: identify a highly fragmented field (such as B2B sales), use AI + middle office thinking to integrate existing tools, and lower barriers to use.

This often leads to Product-Market Fit faster than building an entirely new product from scratch.

用了Clay之后，数据覆盖率升至80%+。

不只是OpenAI，Anthropic、Canva、Ramp这些明星公司都在用同一个工具做销售自动化。这个工具叫Clay，一个看起来像Excel、实际上是"销售工作流操作系统"的产品。

看看融资数据：2025年5月估值15亿美元，8月就翻到31亿美元。3个月翻倍，领投方是Google母公司Alphabet的CapitalG。

一个"给销售团队用的表格工具"，凭什么让硅谷顶级VC抢着投？

产品拆解：这不是CRM，是"销售数据的操作系统"

Clay不是什么：它不是Salesforce那样的CRM，也不是Apollo那样的销售执行工具。

Clay是什么？一个把150+个数据源、AI研究能力、工作流自动化整合到一个类似Excel界面的"数据中台"。

架构拆解

Layer 1：数据聚合层 Clay接入了150+数据提供商：

  • 传统数据库：ZoomInfo、Apollo、Clearbit、Lusha

  • 意向信号：6sense、Koala（监测网站访问、职位变动、融资事件）

  • 用户自有数据：可以接入自己的API Key

核心创新：Waterfall Enrichment（瀑布流丰富）

传统做法是订阅ZoomInfo，找不到数据就算了。Clay的逻辑是：

如果Apollo没找到邮箱 → 自动尝试Hunter → 再试RocketReach → 直到找到为止

Layer 2：AI处理层（核心竞争力）

Claygent - Clay的AI研究Agent，本质是一个可编程的GPT-4网络爬虫。

给它一个prompt："这家公司是否SOC-II认证？"它会：

  1. 1. 自动搜索相关网页

  2. 2. 访问公司官网/安全页面

  3. 3. 提取关键信息

  4. 4. 返回结构化答案

真实使用数据：

  • 30%的Clay用户每天使用Claygent

  • 每天执行50万次AI研究任务

  • 相当于给每个销售团队配了个24小时不睡觉的研究助理

典型场景：判断潜在客户是否B2B公司、抓取最近融资新闻、分析竞品技术栈、从招聘JD里提取公司痛点。

Layer 3：工作流自动化层

Clay的界面看起来像Excel，但底层是一个无代码编程环境。

举个例子：

IF 公司规模 > 500人 
  → 用ZoomInfo查数据（贵但准确）
ELSE 
  → 用Apollo查（便宜）

还能调用任何HTTP API，接入自己的数据源，同时还有Webhook触发：新leads进CRM → 自动触发Clay enrichment流程。

Credit制度 + PLG增长 = 资本疯抢的原因

Clay不按座位收费，而是按消耗的数据credits收费。

为什么不按座位收费？

✅ Unlimited users（无限用户）→ 降低扩张摩擦，整个销售团队都能用

✅ 用多少付多少 → 收入与价值直接挂钩

✅ Credits可滚动 → 降低用户"浪费"焦虑

这是典型的PLG（Product-Led Growth）策略：让用户自然增长，而不是靠销售push。

为什么Google、Sequoia抢着投31亿？

投资价值点

1. 明星客户背书：OpenAI、Anthropic、Canva、Ramp都在用。这些公司对工具的要求极高，选择Clay本身就是信任票。

2. 150+数据源的整合壁垒：接入150+个API不是技术难题，难在维护成本。每个API的格式、鉴权、限流规则都不同，这需要持续的工程投入。

3. Claygent的技术护城河：不只是调用OpenAI API，Clay在prompt engineering和网络爬虫上有独特积累。这是别人短期模仿不了的。

4. GTM赛道的爆发期：GTM（Go-To-Market）自动化是2025年最热的SaaS赛道。为什么？因为AI让"数据驱动销售"从口号变成现实。

5. 从工具到平台的想象空间：Clay已经推出了原生Sequencer（邮件自动化）。未来可能推出CRM，成为"GTM全栈平台"。

3个月估值翻倍的市场信号

2025年5月，Clay估值15亿（Sequoia领投）。

2025年8月，Clay估值31亿（CapitalG领投）。

这背后是什么？

  • AI应用层的"iPhone时刻"到了

  • 从"AI能力"（大模型API）到"AI工作流"（整合多个工具）的范式转移

  • 资本判断：数据中台 > 单点工具

本土化机会：直接复制Clay在中国为什么不行？

最直接的障碍

1. 数据源缺失

  • LinkedIn在中国不可用

  • ZoomInfo/Apollo覆盖中国企业数据很弱

  • 需要整合企查查、天眼查、脉脉等本土数据源

2. B2B成熟度低

  • 中国ToB销售更依赖"关系驱动"而非"数据驱动"

  • Sales Ops岗位在中国企业不成熟

  • 年付$1,800（人民币1.3万）的接受度低

3. 合规风险

  • 自动化抓取数据可能触及《个人信息保护法》

  • 电话/邮箱数据使用受限

可能的切入点

方向1：垂直行业版Clay

  • 针对医药/金融SaaS（这些行业B2B流程更规范）

  • 整合本土数据源：企查查企业信息 + 脉脉职场动态 + 36氪融资数据

  • 年费定价：人民币5,000-8,000元（对标国际版1/3价格）

方向2：出海企业服务

  • 中国SaaS出海热潮（TikTok、Shein带动）

  • 帮中国企业enrichment海外客户数据

  • 反向Clay：连接海外数据源但服务中国团队

方向3：私域运营工具（中国特色）

  • 企微版Clay：自动化打标签、分层运营、内容推送

  • 结合AI生成个性化话术（类似Claygent）

  • 适配中国"私域流量"的玩法

给创业者的启发

  • "中台思维"的机会：整合碎片化工具 > 重新发明轮子：Clay没有自己的数据库，但把150+个数据源整合到一起。这是典型的"中台"打法。对于初创公司来说，不要总想着从0到1做新产品，整合已有工具、降低使用门槛，可能是更快的PMF路径。

  • PLG + Credit制 = 2025年SaaS的标准配置：按座位收费已经过时了。Unlimited users + 按使用量付费，才是符合AI时代的定价模式。如果你在做SaaS，考虑"先让用户用起来，再通过消耗收费"的模式。

  • AI Agent不是噱头，是真正降低人力成本的生产力工具：Claygent每天执行50万次研究任务。这不是"AI辅助"，而是“AI替代”——替代初级研究岗位的重复劳动。别做"AI套壳"产品。真正有价值的AI应用，是让用户少雇1个人、省掉1个工作流。

Clay的本质不是一个"数据库公司"（数据来自第三方），也不是纯"AI公司"（调用OpenAI API），而是一个“销售工作流的操作系统”。

它证明了一件事：AI时代的机会不只在大模型，更在"把AI能力整合到具体工作流"的应用层。

对于创业者，Clay给了一个清晰的方向：找到一个碎片化严重的领域（比如ToB销售），用AI + 中台思维整合现有工具，降低使用门槛。

这比从0做一个"新产品"，可能更快找到Product-Market Fit。
