Company

Liberate robots from the ground.

Founded in 2025 and based in Shanghai, Tesserax — 跃空维度 — pushes embodied intelligence off the floor. The company builds systems that can sense, understand, decide, and act across spatial dimensions, for industrial operations, exploration, rescue, and future aerospace missions.

Reach the work, then do the work

Machines that fly have no hands. Machines with hands cannot fly.

Humanoids, arms, and quadrupeds stay bound to the ground. Drones can fly — but they cannot grasp. Tesserax closes that split: flying manipulators that move like aircraft and work like hands.

Product

From watching the scene to intervening in it.

Tesserax designs flying dexterous-hand systems and a modular aerial platform — combining autonomous flight, multimodal perception, AI decision-making, and physical operation.

01 · High speed

Rocket Dexterous Hand

A high-speed flying hand designed for fast transit and precise grasp — retracting in flight, unfolding to work.

02 · Heavy load

Flying Dexterous Hand

A heavier-duty aerial gripper for rigid objects — built for industrial handling, not just observation.

03 · Platform

Modular flight body

Swappable perception, compute, and task modules on a shared airframe — cameras, ranging, and hands as needed.

04 · Intelligence

Aerial operating stack

An intelligent layer for aerospace-style tasks: maneuver, operate, and decide as one closed loop.

Where it works

Three-dimensional work, not just three-dimensional views.

The low-altitude economy is moving from sensing and transport to autonomous operation. Tesserax focuses on places that need both reach and a hand.

Industry

From inspection to operation

Industrial energy and infrastructure: perceive a complex site, then perform the physical task — not only film it from above.

Logistics

From transport to delivery

Aerial movement paired with precise placement — so cargo is not just carried, but handed off where it needs to go.

Rescue

From detection to execution

Search, reach, and assist in environments that are hard or dangerous for people to enter quickly.

The gap

Flight without hands

Today’s drones are eyes in the sky. They lack the physical interaction needed for repair, grasp, and on-site intervention.

The leap

Flying manipulators

Not a drone bolted to an arm. A single aerial body that can arrive fast and then execute — like a bird that can also use its hands.

Leadership

Hardware craft and embodied algorithms, together.

The founding team combines aircraft-making, industrial design, and VLA-side software — so the flying body and the intelligence stack are designed as one product.

Founder & CEO

Stark Xie

Product, hardware, and the flying-robot thesis.

Former electrical engineer at Siemens, rendering engineer at Chery Automobile, industrial designer at Dreame Technology, and robotics hardware R&D consultant at Qiyuan Robotics. B.E. in Electrical Engineering from Moscow Power Engineering Institute; M.A. in Industrial Design from HSE University. A lifelong aircraft builder who now leads Tesserax’s jump from ground robots into the air.

Co-founder & CTO

Daniel Liu

Multiple awards in algorithm and modeling competitions. Owns the path from perception and decision to executable flight and manipulation.
